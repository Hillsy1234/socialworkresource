Monthly discussions and moderation records

A scheduled Netlify function prepares one fictional scenario on the first of each month at 06:15 UTC. It appears here for approval, not automatically in the public community. The manual button prepares the current month if the schedule has not run. It never duplicates or restores an existing scenario.

Approved posts remain public until removed. Removing a topic hides the complete discussion; removing an individual reply erases that reply’s content. Rejection and removal erase the selected contribution’s text, display name and link. Decision notes and references remain private.

A THOUGHTFUL COMMUNITY

Keep the conversation useful.

Protect people’s information

Use fictional situations and general learning questions. Do not share identifiable case information, names, workplaces, contact details or case documents—even with names changed.

Be clear about your perspective

State your practice location, check original sources and distinguish opinion from guidance. This community is not a service for urgent help, case decisions or legal advice.

Share respectfully

Challenge ideas with care. Avoid harassment, advertising and unsupported claims. Use “Report” if a contribution needs attention.

What happens to my submission?

Contributions are stored privately on Netlify while awaiting review. Approved contributions and display names are public. Use a pseudonym if you prefer; we do not request an email address or verify contributors’ professional status. There are no private messages or document uploads.

Rejected or removed contributions have their text, display name and resource link erased from that item. Moderation records and references are retained to manage the community. Removing a topic also hides its replies; a moderator can separately remove reply text. To request removal, report the contribution and include its reference—never add case details.

We use short-lived form checks and hashed network identifiers to limit spam. Your private CPD notes are not read or shared. Submissions are not saved as drafts in this browser. Please keep a copy of your own non-sensitive text until you receive a submission reference.

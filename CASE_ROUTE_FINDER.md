# Case Route Finder

Use this page when a case feels legally busy and you need to slow the thinking down.

The route finder is designed to help practitioners, students, ASYEs, supervisors, and managers identify which legal frameworks may need attention. It does not make a legal decision for you. It helps you decide what to check next.

## How to Use It

1. Select the case features that appear relevant.
2. Read the suggested routes.
3. Open the linked learning sections.
4. Record which frameworks you considered and why.
5. Use supervision, local procedures, and legal advice for live or contested cases.

## Good Route-Finding Questions

- What does the person want?
- What needs, risks, strengths, and protective factors are present?
- Is there a decision-specific capacity question?
- Is there consent, refusal, coercion, objection, or restriction?
- Is a safeguarding duty engaged?
- Is compulsion for mental health assessment or treatment being considered?
- What is the least restrictive lawful plan?

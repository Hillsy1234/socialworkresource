Adult safeguarding — Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland’s adult safeguarding framework is set out in Adult Safeguarding: Prevention and Protection in Partnership and operational procedures for adults at risk of harm and adults in need of protection. The framework emphasises prevention, early intervention, partnership and access to justice.

An adult at risk of harm is understood through personal characteristics and/or life circumstances that increase exposure to abuse, exploitation or neglect. Consider whether the adult is in need of protection, the person’s wishes, safe communication, advocacy, coercion and immediate safety. Refer through the relevant Health and Social Care Trust and local procedure; do not invent a statutory section 42 threshold.

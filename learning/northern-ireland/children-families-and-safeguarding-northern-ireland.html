Children, families and safeguarding — Northern Ireland

Use Co-operating to Safeguard Children and Young People in Northern Ireland, regional child-protection procedures and the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995. Health and Social Care Trust children’s services, education, health, PSNI and community partners have distinct responsibilities.

Listen to the child, use accessible communication and record what is said separately from adult reports and analysis. Coordinate family support, disability, education and transition. Check the current policy, last update and local Trust procedure before applying a route.

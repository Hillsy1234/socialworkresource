Professional Foundations — Northern Ireland

Use the Northern Ireland Social Care Council (NISCC) standards and employer policies as the professional anchor. Record the person’s voice, rights, communication, evidence, analysis and review. Health and Social Care Trusts, community organisations, PSNI and independent providers may each have distinct roles.

Ask which decision was yours, what information you checked, how the person participated and what supervision or escalation was needed. Do not import Social Work England, Social Care Wales or Scottish terminology into Northern Ireland practice.

Mental health — Northern Ireland

Mental-health law in Northern Ireland is principally governed by the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 and related policy and guidance. Identify the purpose of assessment or treatment, the responsible professionals, the person’s rights, advocacy and review route. Do not substitute the Mental Health Act routes used in England, Wales or Scotland.

Separate mental-health compulsion from physical healthcare, capacity decisions, deprivation of liberty and adult safeguarding. Record alternatives, communication, family or carer views, legal authority and review. Check current Department of Health guidance and local Trust procedures.

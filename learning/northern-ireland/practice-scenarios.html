Scenario Workouts — Northern Ireland practice guide

All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.

Professional Foundations — Northern Ireland

A family receives different explanations from a Trust worker and a commissioned provider about why visits have reduced.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Establish who changed the assessed plan and who changed delivery. Obtain the recorded decision and reasons, hear the person’s experience and address immediate unmet needs. Escalate the appropriate assessment or contract issue instead of referring every complaint to the professional regulator.

Social Care and Support — Northern Ireland

A disabled parent needs morning assistance. Their teenager is missing school to help, and a relative suggests using the parent’s benefits to pay informally.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Assess the parent’s support needs and the impact on the young person. Consider Trust-arranged care or an eligible direct-payment arrangement with proper support. Clarify funding and employment responsibilities; do not assume the child can continue filling a gap in services.

Decision-Making and Capacity — Northern Ireland

A hospital team asks a nominated person to sign consent for an unrelated operation because a deprivation-of-liberty assessment has been completed.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Separate the operation from the liberty arrangements. Check the patient’s ability to decide about that treatment and the applicable consent law. The nominated-person role and a liberty assessment do not provide a general consent signature for another intervention.

Restrictions and Liberty — Northern Ireland

A care setting continues to use an old checklist after June 2026 and says its completed form proves all arrangements are lawful.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Review the actual restrictions and the source version used. Ask the Trust’s MCA team to apply the current guidance and determine any required reassessment or authorisation. Continue meeting immediate care and participation duties while resolving the legal position.

Mental Health — Northern Ireland

A person declines admission after an argument at home but agrees to a same-day assessment and support from a trusted friend.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Explore the immediate safety evidence, physical health and whether voluntary arrangements are workable. Ask the authorised professionals to assess the actual statutory criteria if compulsion is proposed. Refusal of the first offer is not itself the required harm analysis.

Adult Safeguarding — Northern Ireland

An isolated adult says a neighbour takes their pension but fears losing the only person who buys groceries.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Arrange a safe conversation and practical alternatives for food and contact. Assess the protection concern and report through the relevant Trust and police routes as appropriate. A refusal of one intervention does not remove the need to explore coercion, dependency and a safer support plan.

Children, Families and Transitions — Northern Ireland

A child tells a school worker they are frightened to return home. The parent asks for the conversation to remain within school.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Listen without leading questions, record the child’s words and assess immediate safety. Follow the relevant Trust and police referral arrangements where required. Explain confidentiality limits safely and establish who will act before the child leaves the setting.

Rights, Culture and Recording — Northern Ireland

A service offers an assessment in an inaccessible building and says its policy treats every applicant identically.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Identify the disability-related barrier and the applicable adjustment duty. Offer an accessible arrangement and revisit any adverse inference caused by the failed appointment. Record the change and how the person can challenge the earlier decision.

Capacity and decision-making — Northern Ireland

The Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 is partially commenced. Its first phase concerns deprivation of liberty, research and money and valuables; do not assume every provision is in force for every care or treatment decision. Check the Department of Health commencement information, the applicable Code and the age and decision concerned.

Define the decision and time, provide practicable support and record the evidence for the functional test. Identify a nominated person or other representative where relevant. A capacity conclusion does not automatically authorise every restriction; establish the statutory authority and review route.

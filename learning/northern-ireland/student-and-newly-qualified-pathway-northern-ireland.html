Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Explain the purpose of contact, professional role and confidentiality limits in accessible language. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating “the Trust decided” as sufficient explanation without identifying the decision, authority and reasons.

Social Care and Support

Describe the person’s needs and desired outcomes, including fluctuation and the effect of unavailable support. Work through the fictional case and explain: Confusing the carer’s support with services for the cared-for person, or describing an unpaid family arrangement as an agreed funded plan.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Identify the proposed act and confirm that the provision being relied on is commenced for that purpose. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming full commencement or treating the nominated person as a universal decision-maker.

Restrictions and Liberty

Describe actual arrangements and obtain evidence about the person’s wishes, understanding and objections. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating an old definition, a completed form or family approval as a complete legal analysis.

Mental Health

Identify voluntary support and whether an assessment or treatment detention route is actually being considered. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming the 2016 capacity legislation has replaced every mental-health admission provision.

Adult Safeguarding

Identify abuse, exploitation or neglect and the adult’s ability to protect themselves in their actual circumstances. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating a safeguarding policy as a detention power or treating a proposed Bill as commenced law.

Children, Families and Transitions

Record the child’s account, daily experience, harm concerns and support needs. Work through the fictional case and explain: Allowing an internal discussion or a parent’s request for secrecy to replace the required protection response.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the relevant equality law and public-authority duty for the actual decision. Work through the fictional case and explain: Copying an equality test from another legal system or equating identical treatment with accessible participation.

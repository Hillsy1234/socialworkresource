Printable Tools

Use these prompts for learning, supervision, workshops, assessment preparation, and reflective practice.

They are not official forms. They are practice prompts that help you structure your thinking before recording in your employer's system.

How to Use Printable Tools

  1. Choose the prompt that matches the task.
  2. Download or print it.
  3. Adapt it to the person, local policy, and recording system.
  4. Check law, statutory guidance, supervision, and legal advice for live cases.

Care Act Assessment Prompt

Care Act Assessment Prompt

1. Person's own words and outcomes
2. Communication, accessibility, advocacy, and reasonable adjustments
3. Current situation, strengths, networks, and community links
4. Daily living outcomes and impact on wellbeing
5. Carer position and carer's assessment
6. Capacity, consent, and information sharing
7. Risk, safeguarding, and contingency
8. Eligibility analysis
9. Plan, personal budget or direct payment where relevant
10. Review and what would trigger earlier review

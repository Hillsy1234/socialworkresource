Practice glossary

Active Offer

Welsh-language health and care provision offered proactively; check the relevant guidance.

Additional learning needs (ALN)

The Welsh education-support framework; consult the Code for the statutory definition and duties.

Advocacy

Support to help a person communicate and participate. Check which form of advocacy and duties apply.

Consolidation Programme

A Welsh professional development programme for newly qualified social workers; check individual requirements.

CPD

Continuing professional development: learning and reflection to improve practice.

DoLS

Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards. Check Welsh sources and current law for the relevant setting.

Mental capacity

The decision-specific legal framework explored through the MCA and its Code.

Social Care Wales

The source of the Welsh professional Code and registration guidance for social workers.

Adult at risk

The three-part section 126 definition combining abuse/neglect risk, care and support needs and resulting inability to protect oneself.

AMHP

Approved Mental Health Professional: a statutory role in Mental Health Act processes, exercised through the applicable Welsh arrangements.

Best interests

The MCA decision-making process for a person lacking capacity for the relevant decision; includes wishes, values, participation and alternatives.

Care and support plan

A plan translating assessed needs and outcomes into agreed support, responsibilities and review under the Welsh framework.

Care Inspectorate Wales

The Welsh inspectorate relevant to regulated social care services; safeguarding and other agencies may also need involvement.

Care coordinator

The coordinating role within relevant secondary mental health care under the Welsh Measure framework.

Carer assessment

Assessment of the carer’s own support needs and outcomes; family care should not simply be assumed.

Coercion

Pressure or control that may affect the person’s expressed wishes and ability to act freely; requires safe enquiry and careful analysis.

Eligibility

The legal criteria used to determine eligible needs; distinct from the assessment and charging processes.

IDP

Individual development plan under the Welsh additional learning needs framework.

IMCA

Independent Mental Capacity Advocate: a statutory advocacy role for specified circumstances under the MCA framework.

Independent professional advocacy

Advocacy supporting participation under the Welsh social services framework; examine barriers and applicable duties.

Least restrictive option

Consider whether the purpose can be achieved with less interference in rights and freedom.

Mental Health (Wales) Measure 2010

Welsh legislation addressing primary mental health support, secondary care coordination, assessment for eligible former users and advocacy.

Ordinary residence

A legal concept relevant to allocating some social care responsibilities; cross-border cases require specific analysis.

Proportionality

A reasoned comparison of the intended benefit, interference with rights and less intrusive alternatives.

Section 126 enquiry

The Welsh local authority enquiry duty when there is reasonable cause to suspect an adult at risk in its area.

Section 128 reporting

The relevant-partner duty to report an adult-at-risk concern to the local authority where the statutory conditions apply.

Transition

Coordinated preparation for changes in services and adulthood around the young person’s goals and continuity of support.

What matters conversation

An approach to understanding the person’s own outcomes, context, strengths and barriers; it does not replace legal assessment duties.

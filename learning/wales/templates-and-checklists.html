Wales recording templates

Use these ten structures to practise recording facts, analysis, the person’s perspective and agreed action. Download an individual prompt or the full collection below. They are learning aids, not statutory forms. Do not put identifiable case details in this browser.

Care and support assessment

  1. Person’s account, language and communication
  2. What matters: outcomes and changes sought
  3. Strengths, relationships, environment and barriers
  4. Care and support needs and evidence
  5. Support for participation and advocacy
  6. Carer position, willingness and separate assessment
  7. Immediate needs and protective action
  8. Eligibility analysis under Welsh regulations and Code
  9. Options, agreed plan, responsibilities and contingency
  10. Charging or financial assessment addressed separately
  11. Disagreements, reasons, review date and challenge route

Practice source

Carer support assessment

  1. Carer’s own account and desired outcomes
  2. Nature and impact of caring, including work, study and wellbeing
  3. Willingness and ability to continue: do not presume
  4. Current support and unmet support needs
  5. Language, participation and advocacy
  6. Assessment and eligibility analysis
  7. Support options, contingency and emergency contacts
  8. Agreed actions, responsible people and review

Practice source

Decision-specific capacity

  1. Exact decision and time
  2. Reason to assess and relevant impairment or disturbance
  3. All practicable communication and decision support
  4. Relevant information and realistic options
  5. Person’s responses: understand, retain, use or weigh, communicate
  6. How any inability results from impairment or disturbance
  7. Evidence for conclusion and uncertainties
  8. Can the decision wait? Review trigger and date
  9. Next action and separate best interests process if required

Practice source

Best interests decision

  1. Decision, capacity finding and decision-maker
  2. Attorney/deputy/advance decision: validity and scope
  3. Person’s participation, wishes, feelings, beliefs and values
  4. Relevant circumstances and prospect of regaining capacity
  5. People consulted and their views
  6. Options, benefits, burdens and less restrictive alternatives
  7. Decision, rationale and unresolved disagreement
  8. Separate authority for restraint or deprivation of liberty where relevant
  9. Implementation, challenge route and review

Practice source

Adult safeguarding: Wales

  1. Immediate safety or emergency response
  2. Factual concern, source, timing and evidence preserved
  3. Section 126 adult-at-risk criteria: analysis not just a tick
  4. Adult’s account, desired outcomes, communication and advocacy
  5. Safe contact, coercion and capacity questions
  6. Reporting under Welsh procedures and relevant section 128 duty
  7. Local authority enquiries and multi-agency responsibilities
  8. Information-sharing purpose, lawful justification and recipients
  9. Protection plan, person responsible, timescale and review
  10. Reasons for decisions and escalation of unresolved concerns

Practice source

Liberty and restriction review

  1. Setting, age and actual care arrangements
  2. Restrictions: type, intensity, duration and effects
  3. Person’s wishes, feelings, objection and support to express them
  4. Consent analysis under current law distinct from domestic capacity
  5. Current June 2026 judgment and Welsh guidance checked
  6. Less restrictive options considered
  7. DoLS, court or other legal route: advice and reasons
  8. Existing authorisation scope and conditions
  9. Representation, rights to challenge and urgent actions
  10. Review date and changes requiring earlier review

Practice source

Mental health interface discussion

  1. Immediate safety, person’s account and communication needs
  2. Purpose of proposed intervention
  3. Clinical team and AMHP involvement
  4. Less restrictive support and alternatives
  5. Welsh Code and current statutory provision checked
  6. MHA/MCA/DoLS interface and reasons for proposed route
  7. Physical treatment considered separately
  8. Advocacy, rights information, family or carer involvement
  9. Care coordination, discharge and aftercare questions
  10. Responsibilities, escalation and review

Practice source

Child, family and transition planning

  1. Child or young person’s own words and aspirations
  2. Language, communication and participation
  3. Immediate safeguarding concerns and Welsh procedure
  4. Family strengths, support needs and young carer considerations
  5. ALN/IDP, education, health and care planning links
  6. Decisions and capacity framework where applicable
  7. Named coordinator, services and cross-border questions
  8. Actions before transition and continuity arrangements
  9. Carer support, disagreements and advocacy
  10. Review with the young person: dates and accountable people

Practice source

Reflective supervision

  1. Anonymised learning situation and your role
  2. Facts separated from reports and assumptions
  3. Person’s perspective and what influenced your judgement
  4. Competing hypotheses and contrary evidence
  5. Relevant Welsh sources and dates checked
  6. Power, language, equality and ethical tensions
  7. What went well and what needs to change
  8. Feedback and discussion with manager
  9. Action, evidence of impact and next review

Practice source

Chronology and analysis

  1. Date/time of event and date information received
  2. Event, source and direct or reported evidence
  3. Person’s account and impact
  4. Action and rationale at the time
  5. Patterns, protective factors and alternative explanations
  6. Missing or contradictory evidence
  7. Current risk and proportionate action
  8. Responsible person, deadline and review

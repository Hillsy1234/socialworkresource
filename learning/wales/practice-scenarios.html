Scenario Workouts — Wales practice guide

All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.

Professional Foundations

A worker uses an old professional-code handout and an English-only assessment despite a request for Welsh-language support.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Use the current Code, arrange appropriate communication and revisit any assessment conclusions affected by the barrier. Link the change to supervision and feedback from the person.

Care and Support in Wales

An authority says community support meets a need, but the only advertised group has no accessible transport or available places.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check actual availability and accessibility in the eligibility reasoning. Revisit whether authority involvement is required, record the unmet need and agree interim action. A directory listing alone does not demonstrate that the outcome can be achieved.

Mental Capacity Act 2005

An adult refuses a proposed care arrangement after explaining its benefits and drawbacks, while relatives consider the choice too risky.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Explore the reasoning and support needs without treating disagreement as incapacity. If capacity is lacking, identify the exact authority and best-interests process. Keep the person’s wishes and any liberty issue separately recorded.

DoLS and Deprivation of Liberty

A resident is quiet during meetings but repeatedly packs a bag and asks staff to take them home.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Use appropriate communication to understand the request and record how staff respond. Review actual restrictions and consent evidence with the responsible specialist. Calm presentation does not settle the legal question or remove the need to support a challenge.

Mental Health in Wales

An eligible former secondary-service user reports deterioration and is told that only a new GP referral can lead to assessment.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the Part 3 conditions and explain the direct reassessment route where applicable. Arrange an appropriate response to urgent need and record responsibility and follow-up. Do not confuse reassessment rights with a guarantee of a particular service.

Safeguarding Adults in Wales

An adult understands a relative’s financial demands but relies on that person for meals and fears losing contact if they report.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Consider the protection criteria, dependency and safe ways to hear the adult. Arrange immediate practical help and the appropriate report or enquiries. Understanding the financial transaction does not by itself show that the adult can protect themselves.

Children, Families and Transitions

A young person has an IDP but no agreed adult support when leaving school, and each service assumes the plan guarantees the other’s provision.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Separate education duties from adult care assessment and service eligibility. Identify the young person’s goals, obtain named decisions and arrange a coordinated transition. The existence of an IDP does not establish that adult social-care support has been agreed.

Rights, Language and Recording

A Welsh-speaking adult appears confused during an English assessment but communicates clearly when suitable Welsh-language support is arranged.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Revisit the evidence and any capacity or eligibility conclusions affected by the communication barrier. Record the support required for future contacts and the person’s account, rather than retaining an unsupported label of confusion.

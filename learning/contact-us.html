Contact Us

Use this page to contact Daily Mindset Moments CIC about the Social Worker Resource, partnership ideas, AI product development, workflow automation, wellbeing tools, care technology, or community learning platforms.

Daily Mindset Moments CIC is led by Raymond Hill, founder and social worker. The work combines practical AI software development with a social mission: helping people, organisations, families, communities, and professionals do more with less pressure.

The website contact form is handled through Netlify Forms. Website form notifications should be sent to info@daily-mindset-moments.com.

What to Contact Us About

  • feedback, corrections, or improvement ideas for this Social Worker Resource;
  • training, learning, or practice-support resources;
  • AI product builds, prototypes, dashboards, portals, or practical automation;
  • wellbeing, care, safeguarding, family-support, or community technology;
  • partnership conversations with social impact at the centre.

Before You Send

Please do not include confidential case details, names of people using services, addresses, NHS numbers, court documents, or any information that could identify a person in a live social work matter.

For live casework, use your employer's supervision, legal advice, safeguarding procedures, and local escalation routes.

Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Explain your SSSC role, employer and the purpose of involvement; check any specialist appointment required for the work. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating an employer instruction as the complete explanation for a professional decision.

Social Care and Support

Assess personal outcomes and explain which community-care duty and eligibility policy are being applied. Work through the fictional case and explain: Presenting SDS as direct payments only, or treating a relative’s preference as the adult’s choice.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Identify the decision and use communication support before concluding that the adult cannot act or decide. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using a global incapacity label or assuming that any attorney can make every decision.

Restrictions and Liberty

Describe the actual restrictions and the person’s responses, including attempted departures and staff interventions. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming that guardianship automatically authorises every restriction used by a provider.

Mental Health

Identify whether emergency detention, short-term detention or an application for a compulsory treatment order is proposed. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using one detention threshold for every route, or equating refusal with the statutory decision-making criterion.

Adult Safeguarding

Refer concerns on the basis of the available evidence; a referrer need not prove that all criteria are met. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating capacity as proof that an adult can protect themselves, or using protection criteria to deny ordinary services.

Children, Families and Transitions

Use the child’s account and daily-life evidence alongside family strengths and protection concerns. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating GIRFEC as a power to compel cooperation or assuming adult support will begin automatically at transition.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the actual decision and the public body or service responsible for it. Work through the fictional case and explain: Writing that equality was considered without identifying the barrier, adjustment or effect on the decision.

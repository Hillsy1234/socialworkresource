Privacy Policy — Scotland

Learning stored on your device

The app stores your selected location, reading progress, confidence, saved reflections and unfinished reflection fields in this browser. Records are separated by location. They are not submitted to a professional body. Export or print a copy before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Website enquiries

If you use the contact form, the details you submit are sent to the website’s form service and operator. Do not send confidential case information. External source websites have their own privacy policies.

Your records

Use Delete reflection to remove saved entries. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes local progress and notes. Contact Daily Mindset Moments CIC about information sent through an enquiry.

One-Page Legal Map

| Situation | Main framework | First question |

|---|---|---|

| Adult appears to need care/support | Care Act 2014 | Does the person need assessment, information, advice, prevention, advocacy, or services? |

| Carer appears to need support | Care Act 2014 | Is the caring role sustainable and willing? |

| Adult at risk of abuse/neglect | Care Act Section 42 | Are the three safeguarding criteria reasonably suspected? |

| Specific decision capacity concern | Mental Capacity Act 2005 | What is the exact decision and what support has been provided? |

| Best interests decision | Mental Capacity Act 2005 | What would be in this person's best interests, considering wishes, feelings, values, and less restrictive options? |

| Restrictive care in hospital/care home | DoLS / MCA / 2026 DoL test | Does a multifactorial assessment suggest deprivation of liberty? |

| Restrictive care in community | Court of Protection / MCA | Is Article 5 engaged outside DoLS settings? |

| Mental disorder and possible compulsion | Mental Health Act | Is detention for assessment/treatment necessary and lawful? |

| Child in need | Children Act Section 17 | Does the child need services to achieve or maintain health/development? |

| Child protection concern | Children Act Section 47 | Is there reasonable cause to suspect significant harm? |

| Disability/SEND age 0-25 | Children and Families Act / SEND Code | Are education, health, and care needs joined up? |

| Discrimination or adjustments | Equality Act 2010 | What protected characteristic or reasonable adjustment is relevant? |

| Liberty, family life, dignity, life | Human Rights Act | Is interference lawful, necessary, and proportionate? |

Care Act Checklist

  • Appearance of need?
  • Person's outcomes?
  • Communication needs?
  • Advocacy?
  • Carer?
  • Strengths and informal support?
  • Eligible needs?
  • Wellbeing impact?
  • Prevention options?
  • Safeguarding?
  • Capacity/consent?
  • Personal budget/direct payments?
  • Review and contingency?

MCA Checklist

  • What is the decision?
  • Why is capacity in question?
  • What impairment/disturbance is present?
  • What support has been provided?
  • Can the person understand?
  • Can they retain?
  • Can they use/weigh?
  • Can they communicate?
  • Is the conclusion because of the impairment/disturbance?
  • If lacks capacity: best interests, less restrictive options, consultation, record.

Deprivation of Liberty 2026 Screening

Consider:

  • type of restrictions
  • duration
  • effects on person
  • manner of implementation
  • objection
  • wishes and feelings
  • ability to validly consent
  • relative normality
  • purpose
  • setting
  • less restrictive alternatives
  • doubt or borderline status

If in doubt, refer for DoLS or seek legal advice.

Section 42 Safeguarding Threshold

Reasonable cause to suspect:

  1. care and support needs
  2. abuse or neglect, or risk of abuse or neglect
  3. unable to protect self because of those needs

MHA Assessment Orientation

Ask:

  • Is there mental disorder?
  • What is the nature or degree?
  • What risks arise?
  • Why hospital?
  • Why now?
  • Why compulsion?
  • What alternatives were considered?
  • What does the person want?
  • What do family/carers say?
  • What are the equality, cultural, and trauma issues?
  • What aftercare or community support is needed?

Red Flags

  • person cannot be heard privately
  • fear, coercion, or controlling relationship
  • unexplained injury
  • locked doors or restraint
  • sedation used to manage behaviour
  • repeated failed discharges
  • carer burnout
  • child providing inappropriate care
  • self-neglect with fire, nutrition, infestation, or medical risk
  • financial exploitation
  • provider says "we always do it this way"
  • no clear legal basis for restriction

Scenario Workouts — Aotearoa New Zealand practice guide

All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.

Professional Foundations — Aotearoa New Zealand

A service describes a Māori client as disengaged after arranging meetings without the support person they requested.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Ask what made the meetings unsafe or unhelpful and agree how participation should work. Review the earlier interpretation and record the person’s goals and chosen supports. Take the effect of professional and institutional power to supervision, with a concrete change to the next contact.

Social Care and Support — Aotearoa New Zealand

A person with a longstanding disability develops additional support needs after an injury, and two services each suggest the other should pay.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Map the needs and the scope of each scheme, obtain the actual eligibility decisions and coordinate the interface. Record interim support and unresolved funding responsibility. Keep the person’s desired outcomes visible while the agencies resolve the dispute.

Decision-Making and Capacity — Aotearoa New Zealand

A woman can express a clear preference about treatment but needs information presented in short sessions. Her son says he should sign because he manages her bills.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Provide the communication support and assess the relevant decision. Financial help does not establish healthcare authority. Read any appointment and use the applicable consent framework, preserving the woman’s own choices to the extent she can make them.

Restrictions and Liberty — Aotearoa New Zealand

A residential provider uses a locked room when a resident becomes distressed and says the welfare guardian approved the behaviour plan.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Assess immediate safety, the purpose and effect of isolation and the person’s experience. Check the guardian’s powers and the specific legal and service requirements for the measure. Explore communication, environmental and staffing changes, and escalate any missing authority or safeguard.

Mental Health — Aotearoa New Zealand

A team wants to apply a future provision of the 2026 Act because it has been included in a training presentation.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Confirm the commencement date and use the operative legislation and forms for the person’s present assessment. Explain current district-inspector and review rights. Record preparation for the new law as service development, not as the authority for today’s intervention.

Adult Safeguarding — Aotearoa New Zealand

An adult using funded support reports that a worker withholds meals as punishment but fears losing the service if they complain.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Establish immediate access to food and safe support, hear the adult privately and preserve the relevant facts. Follow the appropriate safeguarding and complaint routes and consider criminal duties or police involvement. Plan continuity so reporting does not leave the person without essential assistance.

Children, Families and Transitions — Aotearoa New Zealand

A conference agrees that relatives will support a child, but nobody confirms transport, sleeping arrangements or who will supervise overnight.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Translate the broad agreement into a practical plan with named responsibilities and the child’s view. Verify that the arrangements are available and safe. Use the appropriate review or reconvening process if the plan does not meet the needs identified.

Rights, Culture and Recording — Aotearoa New Zealand

A worker receives sensitive health information from a relative and later receives a police request for the entire record.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the current collection-notification requirements and any applicable exception, then assess disclosure separately. Establish the police request’s purpose and legal basis, share only what the applicable rule permits and document the person’s participation or why authorisation could not appropriately be sought.

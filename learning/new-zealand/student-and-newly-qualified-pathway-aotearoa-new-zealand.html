Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Confirm registration, practising status, competence and the authority required for the task. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating whānau involvement as either automatically required in every decision or automatically excluded by individual consent.

Social Care and Support

Identify the responsible funder and referral route, including NASC or another applicable service. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming that one diagnosis, one assessment or one funding approval covers every service need.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Specify the treatment or other decision and provide the support needed for participation. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating diminished competence as loss of every choice or assuming financial authority includes healthcare.

Restrictions and Liberty

Describe actual restrictions and the person’s wishes, including what happens when they want to leave. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating a care plan, guardian’s agreement or disability diagnosis as universal authority for confinement.

Mental Health

Check whether the operative 1992 route or a subsequently commenced provision applies. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating the 2026 Act as fully operational before its commencement.

Adult Safeguarding

Identify immediate danger, the adult’s wishes and a safe way to communicate. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming a provider incident report resolves all legal duties or covers safety outside the provider’s remit.

Children, Families and Transitions

Record the concern and use the appropriate Oranga Tamariki or police route where needed. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating agreement at a family meeting as proof that a safe, authorised and deliverable plan exists.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the consumer right, equality issue or public-function decision affecting the person. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using an outdated privacy-code version or treating collection and disclosure as the same legal decision.

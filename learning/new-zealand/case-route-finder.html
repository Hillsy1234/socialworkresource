Case Route Finder — Aotearoa New Zealand

Use the route finder

Select the concerns that fit a fictional case. More than one framework may be relevant. Open each suggested module and its sources; the tool selects learning topics and does not decide a legal threshold.

Compare the routes

Separate support needs, decision-making, mental-health intervention, protective reporting and restrictions. Record the question each route answers, the source you checked and who needs to provide further advice.

Route questions

Care or carer support needs

Which needs and outcomes belong to the person and to carers?

Identify the responsible funder and referral route, including NASC or another applicable service. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

A decision-specific capacity question

What is the precise decision and why is ability to decide questioned?

Specify the treatment or other decision and provide the support needed for participation. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Restrictions or liberty concerns

What restrictions actually operate and how do they affect the person?

Describe actual restrictions and the person’s wishes, including what happens when they want to leave. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Mental health support or compulsion questions

What is the purpose of each proposed intervention?

Check whether the operative 1992 route or a subsequently commenced provision applies. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Abuse, neglect or safeguarding concerns

How can the person be heard safely and privately?

Identify immediate danger, the adult’s wishes and a safe way to communicate. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

A child, young carer or transition issue

What does the child say or show about safety and daily life?

Record the concern and use the appropriate Oranga Tamariki or police route where needed. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Language, participation or recording concerns

What makes participation accessible for this person?

Identify the consumer right, equality issue or public-function decision affecting the person. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Professional learning or supervision

What is the purpose and limit of your role?

Confirm registration, practising status, competence and the authority required for the task. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

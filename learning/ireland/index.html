Modules

01 Professional Foundations — Ireland Explain your professional role, distinguish an ethical concern from a statutory power, and turn a formulation into an accountable plan. 02 Social Care and Support — Ireland Map assessment, eligibility, funding and delivery separately; include family support without assuming it; explain the local route for challenging a decision. 03 Decision-Making and Capacity — Ireland Identify the precise decision, provide practical decision support, apply the local capacity or consent framework and verify a representative’s authority. 04 Restrictions and Liberty — Ireland Describe actual restrictions, identify the setting-specific legal authority, test alternatives and make representation and review practical. 05 Mental Health — Ireland Distinguish voluntary care, compulsory assessment, detention and treatment; connect the local criteria to evidence and explain the person’s review rights. 06 Adult Safeguarding — Ireland Recognise abuse, neglect and coercion; choose the correct local reporting route; connect immediate protection to the adult’s wishes and continuing support. 07 Children, Families and Transitions — Ireland Separate family support from protection powers, apply the local reporting duty, involve the child and coordinate education, health and transition planning. 08 Rights, Culture and Recording — Ireland Connect rights to an actual service decision, distinguish privacy from absolute secrecy and write a record that makes reasons and disagreement visible.

Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Confirm your professional role, competence and any specialist authority needed. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using another profession’s Code or assuming that an employer policy creates a statutory power.

Social Care and Support

Identify daily-living needs and which HSE service or other body is responsible for the requested support. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating allocation of funding as evidence that a service has started, or conflating home-support and nursing-home schemes.

Decision-Making and Capacity

State the precise decision and provide accessible information and communication support. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating every DSS arrangement as substitute decision-making or replacing will and preferences with professional convenience.

Restrictions and Liberty

Describe what happens if the person asks to leave and record their will and preferences. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using consent to admission, a diagnosis or a relative’s signature as blanket authority to prevent departure.

Mental Health

Check the commencement record and current MHC operational guidance before choosing forms, criteria or time limits. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming that Royal Assent or publication means that every replacement procedure is already operational.

Adult Safeguarding

Identify the concern and safe contact arrangements, including coercion and dependency. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating a national policy as a universal coercive power or overlooking service continuity after a report.

Children, Families and Transitions

Establish whether you are a mandated person and whether the concern meets the statutory reporting threshold. Work through the fictional case and explain: Equating the mandated-report threshold with the only circumstances in which Tusla should receive a concern.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the right or equality duty engaged by the actual service decision. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating public-sector status as unrestricted permission to share records.

Professional Foundations — Ireland

Use CORU’s Social Workers Registration Board Code of Professional Conduct and Ethics as the professional anchor. Registered social workers must follow the statutory Code and maintain continuing professional development. HSE, Tusla, disability services and community organisations may have different roles and procedures.

Record the person’s voice, communication, rights, evidence, analysis, professional judgement and review. This is an Ireland learning guide, not a UK regulator or legal route.

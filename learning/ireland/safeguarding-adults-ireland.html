Safeguarding adults — Ireland

Use current HSE safeguarding policy and local safeguarding and protection teams for adults who may experience abuse, exploitation, neglect or harm. Establish the person’s account, immediate safety, communication, consent, decision-support needs, advocacy and desired outcomes.

Information-sharing and referral routes depend on the HSE service and circumstances. Follow the current national policy and local procedure, record the lawful reason for sharing and do not invent a UK statutory threshold.

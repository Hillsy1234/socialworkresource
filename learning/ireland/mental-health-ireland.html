Mental health — Ireland

The Mental Health Act 2001 and Mental Health Commission standards and guidance govern approved-centre admission, treatment safeguards, advocacy and tribunal review. Identify the purpose of intervention, the person’s rights, independent advocacy, responsible professionals and review route.

Separate mental-health detention from physical healthcare, decision-support arrangements, restrictions and safeguarding. Check current Mental Health Commission guidance and local HSE procedures; do not import Mental Health Act sections from any UK jurisdiction.

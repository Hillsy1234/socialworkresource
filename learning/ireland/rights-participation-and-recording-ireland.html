Rights, participation and recording — Ireland

Use CORU standards, equality and human-rights duties, accessible communication, advocacy and HSE or Tusla complaints routes. Record the person’s wishes, support offered, legal basis, alternatives, proportionality, disagreement and review.

Check whether information sharing is necessary, proportionate and lawful. Explain decisions in a way the person can understand and keep Irish professional and organisational pathways distinct from UK systems.

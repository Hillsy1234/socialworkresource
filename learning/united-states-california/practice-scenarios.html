All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.

Professional Foundations — California

An associate is asked to provide unsupervised clinical services because a manager believes the social-work degree is sufficient.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the registration and supervision rules for the setting and arrange a compliant service. Explain the practitioner’s status accurately and escalate any pressure to exceed scope. Keep urgent support within the role while ensuring the appropriate licensed oversight.

Social Care and Support — California

A recipient’s hours are reduced while their support needs have increased, and the family is told only to find a more efficient worker.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Obtain the assessment and Notice of Action, compare authorised tasks with current evidence and assist with the appropriate hearing request if wanted. Address immediate care gaps separately. Staffing difficulties should not obscure a dispute about the assessment itself.

Decision-Making and Capacity — California

A relative with financial power of attorney signs for treatment while the patient’s healthcare directive names a different agent.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the patient’s own capacity for the treatment, then the directive and the agent’s operative authority. The financial document does not settle healthcare consent. Resolve the discrepancy through the responsible clinical and legal process before treating the signature as valid authority.

Restrictions and Liberty — California

A facility says a person on an assessment hold cannot object to any medication or restraint because they are already detained.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Identify the separate authority and safeguards for each intervention. Explain the person’s rights and connect them with the appropriate advocate or attorney. Review necessity and less restrictive options; the hold does not supply a universal treatment or restraint permission.

Mental Health — California

A team uses an old training sheet to exclude personal safety and necessary medical care from a grave-disability assessment.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the current section 5008 definition and DHCS SB 43 guidance. Record the evidence connecting the qualifying condition to the inability to meet the relevant needs, consider available support and preserve the person’s review rights. The expanded definition still requires an individual legal assessment.

Adult Safeguarding — California

A worker reports suspected financial abuse to a supervisor and assumes that an internal form satisfies the legal duty.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check section 15630 and the setting-specific reporting route, make the required external report and document timing and recipient. Address the adult’s access to money and essential support. Internal reporting can support compliance but does not replace it.

Children, Families and Transitions — California

A child discloses abuse and a manager directs the worker to wait for internal approval before calling the receiving agency.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Apply the reporting threshold and act within the statutory procedure without allowing internal approval to delay the duty. Record the child’s words, the report and immediate safety actions. Avoid conducting a separate investigation to obtain proof before reporting.

Rights, Culture and Recording — California

A court-related request seeks a client’s substance-use treatment record, and a worker relies on a general treatment-sharing consent.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check whether Part 2 applies and what the request, consent and current law permit for proceedings. Consider California requirements separately and seek the necessary advice. Document the disclosure decision and limit any release to what is lawfully authorised.

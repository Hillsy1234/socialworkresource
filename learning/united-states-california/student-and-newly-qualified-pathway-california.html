Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Confirm licence or registration status, practice setting and required supervision. Work through the fictional case and explain: Equating a degree, associate registration and independent clinical licensure.

Social Care and Support

Apply through the appropriate county and identify the current eligibility and health-certification requirements. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating worker availability as proof that the assessed need has disappeared.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Identify the specific healthcare, personal or financial decision and support the person’s participation. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming financial authority, next-of-kin status or a conservatorship label answers every consent question.

Restrictions and Liberty

Identify the actual restriction, setting and the legal measure relied on. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating detention, medication capacity and restraint authorisation as a single decision.

Mental Health

Identify the authorised person, proposed route and evidence for the current statutory criteria. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using a superseded grave-disability definition or assuming all social workers can initiate a hold.

Adult Safeguarding

Identify the person’s statutory category, your reporting role, the alleged harm and the setting. Work through the fictional case and explain: Confusing APS service eligibility with statutory reporting definitions or treating an internal report as sufficient.

Children, Families and Transitions

Identify your mandated-reporter role and the facts supporting knowledge or reasonable suspicion. Work through the fictional case and explain: Waiting for certainty or a manager’s permission before making a required CANRA report.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the organisation, service and applicable state and federal rights or privacy duties. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming HIPAA applies to every record or that a general consent covers all uses in legal proceedings.

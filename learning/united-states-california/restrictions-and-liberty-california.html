Restrictions and Liberty — California

California context

Use the decision-making and mental-health sources below to identify the authority, if any, for the actual restriction. A learning topic called restrictions and liberty does not imply that an overseas authorisation scheme applies here.

Practice enquiry

Restrictions, legal authority, less restrictive options and review. Start with the person’s account and distinguish information received from observations and interpretation.

  • What restrictions actually operate and how do they affect the person?
  • What authority is being relied upon?
  • How can the person challenge the arrangements?

Recording and review

  1. Actual restriction and setting.
  2. Purpose, duration and effects.
  3. Wishes, consent and objections.
  4. Authority and its limits.
  5. Less restrictive alternatives.
  6. Independent support and review.

Explain the reasons for the proposed action, record disagreement and identify who will review the outcome. Check the local legal criteria before treating any learning prompt as a duty or power.

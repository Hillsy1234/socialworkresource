Case Route Finder — California

Use the route finder

Select the concerns that fit a fictional case. More than one framework may be relevant. Open each suggested module and its sources; the tool selects learning topics and does not decide a legal threshold.

Compare the routes

Separate support needs, decision-making, mental-health intervention, protective reporting and restrictions. Record the question each route answers, the source you checked and who needs to provide further advice.

Route questions

Care or carer support needs

Which needs and outcomes belong to the person and to carers?

Apply through the appropriate county and identify the current eligibility and health-certification requirements. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

A decision-specific capacity question

What is the precise decision and why is ability to decide questioned?

Identify the specific healthcare, personal or financial decision and support the person’s participation. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Restrictions or liberty concerns

What restrictions actually operate and how do they affect the person?

Identify the actual restriction, setting and the legal measure relied on. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Mental health support or compulsion questions

What is the purpose of each proposed intervention?

Identify the authorised person, proposed route and evidence for the current statutory criteria. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Abuse, neglect or safeguarding concerns

How can the person be heard safely and privately?

Identify the person’s statutory category, your reporting role, the alleged harm and the setting. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

A child, young carer or transition issue

What does the child say or show about safety and daily life?

Identify your mandated-reporter role and the facts supporting knowledge or reasonable suspicion. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Language, participation or recording concerns

What makes participation accessible for this person?

Identify the organisation, service and applicable state and federal rights or privacy duties. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Professional learning or supervision

What is the purpose and limit of your role?

Confirm licence or registration status, practice setting and required supervision. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

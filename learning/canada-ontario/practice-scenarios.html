Scenario Workouts — Ontario practice guide

All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.

Professional Foundations — Ontario

A manager asks a registered social worker to complete a statutory assessment requiring a particular assessor designation.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the legal requirements and the worker’s actual designation, competence and scope. Arrange an appropriately authorised assessor if needed. Continue gathering relevant social evidence without representing that work as the statutory determination.

Social Care and Support — Ontario

An adult’s home-care visits are reduced although their mobility has worsened. The family is told only to complain to the visiting agency.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Establish who reduced the allocation and obtain the reassessment and reasons. Address immediate safety with the coordinator and use the relevant service-decision review or appeal. Pursue a provider complaint separately if the agency also failed to deliver the authorised visits.

Decision-Making and Capacity — Ontario

A hospital patient understands a proposed treatment but has a property guardian. Staff ask the guardian to consent without assessing treatment capacity.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Assess the patient’s ability for the treatment decision and provide support. Read the guardian’s powers and identify the correct HCCA route only if substitute consent is needed. Financial guardianship is not evidence that the patient cannot consent to treatment.

Restrictions and Liberty — Ontario

A long-term-care home says a daughter’s admission consent also permits a standing order preventing her father from leaving.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Read the admission and representative documents, assess the father’s wishes and the actual restriction, and identify the separate legal basis relied on. Check the current restraint and confinement provisions rather than assuming that admission consent answers the liberty question.

Mental Health — Ontario

A patient is held on Form 1 and a team assumes that this both permits medication without consent and authorises a longer stay.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the assessment period and whether a further lawful admission status is required. Apply HCCA consent separately to the proposed medication. Explain rights and arrange the relevant advice; neither the assessment form nor clinical preference resolves all three decisions.

Adult Safeguarding — Ontario

A worker hears about financial misuse affecting a retirement-home resident and is told to report only to the home’s manager.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the statutory reporting duty and RHRA route; internal reporting may not satisfy it. Address immediate access to funds and support, record the adult’s account and consider any separate police or representative concern.

Children, Families and Transitions — Ontario

A worker raises a new concern about a 12-year-old and a manager says an earlier report by another worker covers it.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Assess the new reasonable grounds and make the required direct report. Record what is new, the child’s account and immediate safety arrangements. An earlier report does not remove the ongoing personal duty.

Rights, Culture and Recording — Ontario

A social worker in a community organisation assumes they can share a complete file using a hospital’s implied-consent practice.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Identify the organisation’s legal status, purpose of disclosure and applicable law. Do not import the hospital’s framework without checking. Obtain or establish the proper basis, limit the disclosure and explain the person’s choices and complaint route.

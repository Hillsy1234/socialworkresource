Mental Health — Ontario

Ontario context

Locate the Mental Health Act and relevant consent legislation. Identify the assessment or admission route, rights advice and review process; admission status and consent to treatment require separate analysis.

Practice enquiry

Mental-health support, crisis response, treatment and rights. Start with the person’s account and distinguish information received from observations and interpretation.

  • What is the purpose of each proposed intervention?
  • Who can assess or exercise the relevant power?
  • What voluntary or less restrictive support is available?

Recording and review

  1. Immediate health and safety needs.
  2. Communication and cultural support.
  3. Purpose of each intervention.
  4. Legal basis and authorised professional.
  5. Rights information and advocacy.
  6. Follow-up and review.

Explain the reasons for the proposed action, record disagreement and identify who will review the outcome. Check the local legal criteria before treating any learning prompt as a duty or power.

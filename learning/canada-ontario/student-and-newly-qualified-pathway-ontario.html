Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Confirm registration, professional scope and any additional authority required. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating College registration as proof of every specialist assessment qualification.

Social Care and Support

Identify the actual programme and responsible assessment or funding body. Work through the fictional case and explain: Confusing a complaint about a worker with an appeal against the amount of funded care.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Specify the decision and identify which Act and decision-maker apply. Work through the fictional case and explain: Equating financial incapacity with treatment incapacity or ignoring applicable prior capable wishes.

Restrictions and Liberty

Identify the actual restriction, setting, legal status and the person’s wishes. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating consent to placement as blanket consent to confinement or relying on an uncommenced amendment.

Mental Health

Identify the exact assessment or admission authority and the evidence supporting its criteria. Work through the fictional case and explain: Confusing an assessment hold, involuntary admission and authority to treat.

Adult Safeguarding

Identify the adult’s setting and the specific suspected abuse, neglect, unlawful conduct or financial misuse. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using one adult-safeguarding process for community, retirement-home and long-term-care settings.

Children, Families and Transitions

Identify the child’s age, concern and reasonable grounds under the CYFSA. Work through the fictional case and explain: Delegating the report to a manager or applying the under-16 mandatory rule without checking the young person’s age.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the service decision, relevant protected ground and accommodation required. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming every social worker is operating under the same health-information consent rules.

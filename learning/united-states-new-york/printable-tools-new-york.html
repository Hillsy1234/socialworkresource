Download and use the tools

The ten prompts cover eight practice topics, supervision and checking sources. Use them individually or export the collection from Templates and Checklists.

Before sharing

Check that the location is New York, use anonymised or fictional information, and keep personal learning notes separate from service records. These prompts help organise reasoning; they do not establish legal authority or meet a regulator’s submission requirements automatically.

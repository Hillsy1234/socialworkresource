Scenario Workouts — New York practice guide

All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.

Professional Foundations — New York

An LMSW is offered an independent psychotherapy caseload without the supervision required for clinical work.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the scope, setting and supervision requirements before accepting the arrangement. Arrange appropriate oversight or a lawful alternative and explain the practitioner’s status accurately. A social-work licence does not mean that both licence categories have identical clinical authority.

Social Care and Support — New York

A person receives an assessment but the plan authorises fewer hours than requested, assuming a relative will provide the rest.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the assessment evidence, the relative’s voluntarily offered assistance and the plan’s written reasons. Identify the appropriate appeal or hearing process and interim safety arrangements. Do not treat an assumed family contribution as available care.

Decision-Making and Capacity — New York

A patient’s adult child assumes they can decide because of family status, while a healthcare proxy names a close friend.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Assess the patient’s own decision-making ability and check the proxy’s operation and scope. Do not jump to the default surrogate list. If a separate guardianship concern exists, identify its functional evidence and alternatives rather than using it to bypass the healthcare arrangement.

Restrictions and Liberty — New York

A facility says a guardian’s authority to choose residence allows it to admit the adult involuntarily to a psychiatric hospital.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Read the order and Article 81 limits, then identify whether Article 9 criteria and procedures are met. Obtain appropriate legal and clinical advice and consider voluntary alternatives. Residence powers should not be used as a substitute for psychiatric admission safeguards.

Mental Health — New York

A team assumes emergency admission requires an express threat and ignores evidence that mental illness prevents a person obtaining essential medical care.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Apply the current statutory definition and assess the connection to substantial physical harm, rather than relying on a threat-only summary. Consider realistic support and alternatives, record each criterion and explain the person’s rights and review route.

Adult Safeguarding — New York

An adult cannot obtain food or manage money because of impairment, and the only relative listed as a contact is unwilling to assist.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Assess the actual availability and reliability of help against the protective-service conditions. Arrange immediate practical support and the appropriate APS response. A name on a contact form is not evidence that someone can responsibly meet the need.

Children, Families and Transitions — New York

A social worker hears a disclosure but is told to wait for a manager to decide whether it is sufficiently certain to report.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Assess reasonable cause under the statute and act through the required procedure. Record the relationship and facts that determine the correct recipient. Do not require certainty or delay a required report for an internal meeting.

Rights, Culture and Recording — New York

A mental-health service receives a broad request for records and assumes HIPAA permission alone resolves all confidentiality requirements.

  • Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
  • What evidence and support could change the next step?
  • How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?

Discussion

Check the record’s coverage under state mental-hygiene law and any Part 2 restrictions as well as HIPAA. Identify the actual request and lawful basis, limit disclosure and explain the person’s rights where appropriate. One applicable rule does not remove another.

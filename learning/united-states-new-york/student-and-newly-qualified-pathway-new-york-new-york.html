Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Confirm the licence, current registration, scope and supervision required for the actual work. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating LMSW and LCSW practice permissions as interchangeable.

Social Care and Support

Identify Medicaid eligibility, the service requested and the responsible assessment and authorisation bodies. Work through the fictional case and explain: Confusing NYIAP assessment with final service authorisation or overlooking current minimum-needs and legacy rules.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Specify the decision, support the person and identify the applicable healthcare or guardianship framework. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating family status as priority over every valid appointment or using diagnosis alone to establish Article 81 incapacity.

Restrictions and Liberty

Describe the restriction, setting, person’s wishes and current legal status. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating a guardian’s placement power as a universal psychiatric detention power.

Mental Health

Identify the exact Article 9 route and evidence for its current criteria. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using an outdated threat-only account of serious harm or confusing the time limits of different admission routes.

Adult Safeguarding

Identify the harm, functional difficulties and whether responsible assistance is actually available. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming that any listed relative rules out APS support or that a referral automatically authorises guardianship.

Children, Families and Transitions

Identify the reporter role, child’s age, alleged responsible person and facts supporting reasonable cause. Work through the fictional case and explain: Confusing reasonable cause with proof, or assuming the SCR is the only possible route for every threat to a child.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the organisation, record and state or federal privacy provisions involved. Work through the fictional case and explain: Checking HIPAA while overlooking state mental-hygiene or specially protected substance-use records.

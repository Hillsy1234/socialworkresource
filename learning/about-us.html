About Us

Daily Mindset Moments CIC is the organisation behind this learning resource. It was founded by Raymond Hill, founder and social worker, with a focus on practical technology that reduces pressure, supports clearer thinking, and helps people turn complex work into usable systems.

This Social Worker Resource reflects that mission. It is designed to help social workers, students, ASYEs, managers, safeguarding practitioners, and care professionals learn key legal frameworks in a more structured, calm, and usable way.

Our Mission

The mission of Daily Mindset Moments is to build practical digital tools with human purpose. The work is centred on helping people, businesses, families, communities, and professionals do more with less pressure.

The aim is not to replace human judgement. It is to give people better systems, clearer information, and more space for care, creativity, decision-making, and meaningful human connection.

For social work, that matters. Good tools should make legal literacy, reflective practice, safeguarding thinking, recording, supervision, and learning easier to access without taking the human voice out of the work.

Why This Resource Exists

Social work law and guidance can feel heavy when it is scattered across legislation, statutory guidance, codes of practice, policy updates, and local procedures. This resource brings the major frameworks into one structured learning space.

It is built to support:

  • clear learning routes through the Care Act, Mental Capacity Act, DoLS, Mental Health Act, safeguarding, transitions, equality, human rights, and recording;
  • practical case analysis that keeps the person, their rights, and their outcomes at the centre;
  • confident supervision, reflective learning, ASYE development, and practice conversations;
  • safer decision-making by encouraging practitioners to check current law, guidance, local policy, and legal advice.

This resource is not legal advice. It is a learning and practice-support tool.

Responsible Technology

Daily Mindset Moments is built around the idea that useful technology should be practical, explainable, privacy-aware, and led by human judgement.

The products are shaped around:

  • human review where decisions affect trust, care, safety, or reputation;
  • accessible design that reduces confusion and cognitive load;
  • responsible use of AI where the person remains in control;
  • practical outcomes such as time saved, clearer communication, better access, and reduced avoidable strain;
  • reinvesting success into wider access to useful AI coaching, skills, and development tools.

Raymond Hill

Raymond Hill is the founder of Daily Mindset Moments CIC and a social worker. His work combines social work values, lived understanding of pressure and cognitive load, and practical AI product development.

The aim is to create tools that feel usable in real life: calm enough to learn from, structured enough to support decision-making, and human enough to keep care, rights, relationships, and dignity at the centre.

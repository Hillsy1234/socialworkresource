Learning Flashcards

Use these flashcards for quick revision, group teaching, supervision warm-ups, ASYE study, university preparation, interview practice, or a short refresher before reading a module in full.

The cards are grouped by module. Choose a module, read the prompt, then flip the card to check the answer.

Study Tips

  • Start with "All Modules" to test broad recall.
  • Use one module at a time before supervision, placement learning, or workshops.
  • Say the answer out loud before flipping the card.
  • Write down any card that feels uncertain and revisit the full module.
  • Use the cards as prompts for case discussion, not as legal advice.

These flashcards summarise learning points from the resource. For live cases, always check current law, statutory guidance, local policy, legal advice, and supervision.

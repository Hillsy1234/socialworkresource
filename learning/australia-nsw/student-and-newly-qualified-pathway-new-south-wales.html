Student and Newly Qualified Pathway — New South Wales

Plan your learning

Work through the eight activities with a practice educator or supervisor. Use fictional examples, identify sources and explain your reasoning before looking at the worked scenario.

Evidence of development

For each activity record one learning point, feedback received and a change to try. These exercises do not replace local qualification, placement, registration or continuing-development requirements. Consult Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW) and your education provider for those requirements.

Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Using a fictional New South Wales case: What is the purpose and limit of your role? Which professional standard informs the next action? How will supervision test your reasoning?

Social Care and Support

Using a fictional New South Wales case: Which needs and outcomes belong to the person and to carers? Which service actually assesses and funds this support? What happens if the proposed support is unavailable?

Decision-Making and Capacity

Using a fictional New South Wales case: What is the precise decision and why is ability to decide questioned? What communication support would help? Who has authority for this decision under the applicable law?

Restrictions and Liberty

Using a fictional New South Wales case: What restrictions actually operate and how do they affect the person? What authority is being relied upon? How can the person challenge the arrangements?

Mental Health

Using a fictional New South Wales case: What is the purpose of each proposed intervention? Who can assess or exercise the relevant power? What voluntary or less restrictive support is available?

Adult Safeguarding

Using a fictional New South Wales case: How can the person be heard safely and privately? Which reporting pathway applies to this person and setting? What immediate action and follow-up are required?

Children, Families and Transitions

Using a fictional New South Wales case: What does the child say or show about safety and daily life? Which reporting duty and receiving agency apply? How will education, health and family support coordinate?

Rights, Culture and Recording

Using a fictional New South Wales case: What makes participation accessible for this person? What legal basis permits disclosure of this information? How will disagreement and reasons be recorded?

Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Identify the employer, state, service setting and any appointment required for the task. Work through the fictional case and explain: Equating association membership, clinical experience or a job title with every statutory appointment.

Social Care and Support

Identify the scheme responsible for each need and the evidence required for its assessment. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating NDIS eligibility as approval for every requested service, or using obsolete aged-care programme rules.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Support the patient’s own decision and establish the precise treatment and capacity question. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating next of kin as the automatic decision-maker or ignoring an objection because a relative agrees.

Restrictions and Liberty

Identify the practice, its purpose and whether it is a regulated restrictive practice in the actual setting. Work through the fictional case and explain: Equating a behaviour support plan with all state approvals, or applying disability-service rules to every hospital or care setting.

Mental Health

Identify the proposed route and connect the actual evidence to its criteria. Work through the fictional case and explain: Confusing mentally ill and mentally disordered routes or treating a diagnosis and refusal as the whole detention test.

Adult Safeguarding

Arrange a safe conversation and identify immediate danger, dependency and the adult’s desired outcomes. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming that every adult-safety concern belongs to one agency or that reporting creates substitute decision-making powers.

Children, Families and Transitions

Check the child’s age, your role and the applicable reporting threshold under the Act. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating a support referral or an internal incident entry as the required child-protection report.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the state or Commonwealth duty relevant to access, discrimination or the proposed intervention. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming that working with the same person creates unrestricted authority to share health records.

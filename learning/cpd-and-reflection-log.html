CPD and Reflection Log

Use this private learning space to draft CPD reflections in a format that is aligned with Social Work England's CPD recording expectations.

Your notes are stored in your own browser on this device. They are not sent to a server and there is no database behind this tool.

They should remain after a normal page refresh, but they can disappear if browser data is cleared, private browsing is used, workplace systems reset storage, or you change device or browser. Export the text file or use Print / Save as PDF if you need to keep a reliable copy.

This tool does not submit CPD to Social Work England. When you are happy with an entry, copy the final wording into your Social Work England online account.

Social Work England CPD Shape

Social Work England expects registered social workers to record a minimum of 2 different pieces of CPD each registration year, with at least 1 piece including peer reflection. Each CPD piece is recorded separately in the online account.

The online form asks you to describe what you have learnt, reflect on the positive impact the CPD has had or will have on your practice and the people you work with, and select which parts of CPD standard 4 apply.

Social Work England's recording guidance suggests around 250 to 500 words for the main learning and impact responses.

Safe Reflection

Keep reflections anonymised. Do not enter names, addresses, dates of birth, case numbers, or identifiable case details.

Useful prompts:

  • What did I learn?
  • Which part of CPD standard 4 does this best evidence?
  • How will this improve my practice, judgement, recording, or confidence?
  • How could this benefit people with lived experience, carers, families, colleagues, students, or the profession?
  • What did peer reflection add to my thinking?
  • What do I need to check, discuss, practise, or record next?

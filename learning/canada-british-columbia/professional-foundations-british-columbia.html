Professional Foundations — British Columbia

British Columbia context

British Columbia College of Social Workers (BCCSW) provides the professional reference for this guide. Check the requirements for the specific registration category, scope and employer.

Practice enquiry

Professional role, ethics, competence, supervision and accountable decisions. Start with the person’s account and distinguish information received from observations and interpretation.

  • What is the purpose and limit of your role?
  • Which professional standard informs the next action?
  • How will supervision test your reasoning?

Recording and review

  1. Role and service purpose.
  2. Person’s goals and consent.
  3. Professional standard and evidence.
  4. Alternative explanations and bias.
  5. Supervision, action and review.

Explain the reasons for the proposed action, record disagreement and identify who will review the outcome. Check the local legal criteria before treating any learning prompt as a duty or power.

Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Confirm the practitioner’s role, regulatory position and any specific statutory designation. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming every social-work role has identical registration requirements or investigative powers.

Social Care and Support

Contact the appropriate health-authority home and community care service for eligibility and needs assessment. Work through the fictional case and explain: Applying general eligibility criteria without checking exceptions or treating an assessment as guaranteed service delivery.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Support the adult’s own decision and apply the relevant statutory incapability test. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using the temporary substitute list without checking an existing representative, or treating an agreement as unlimited authority.

Restrictions and Liberty

Describe the restriction, who enforces it and the adult’s wishes or objections. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating a protection referral or admission consent as authority for ongoing confinement.

Mental Health

Identify the route and evidence for each applicable criterion, including voluntary alternatives. Work through the fictional case and explain: Reducing certification to immediate dangerousness alone or importing another province’s consent rules.

Adult Safeguarding

Identify the alleged harm and why the adult may be unable to seek assistance themselves. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating decisional incapability as the only reason an adult may qualify for assistance.

Children, Families and Transitions

Identify the concern and the child’s age, using the CFCSA reporting threshold. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using another province’s reporting age or assuming that every Indigenous child’s case follows an identical provincial route.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the service decision, relevant right and responsible organisation. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming public-body and private-organisation privacy rules are interchangeable.

Quick Reference — British Columbia

Before choosing an action

  1. Hear the person and clarify immediate safety.
  2. Identify the specific decision, service, setting and jurisdiction.
  3. Check authority, consent, reporting duties and privacy.
  4. Compare support and less restrictive alternatives.
  5. Record evidence, the person’s views, action owners and review.

Topic routes

  • Professional Foundations: What is the purpose and limit of your role?
  • Social Care and Support: Which needs and outcomes belong to the person and to carers?
  • Decision-Making and Capacity: What is the precise decision and why is ability to decide questioned?
  • Restrictions and Liberty: What restrictions actually operate and how do they affect the person?
  • Mental Health: What is the purpose of each proposed intervention?
  • Adult Safeguarding: How can the person be heard safely and privately?
  • Children, Families and Transitions: What does the child say or show about safety and daily life?
  • Rights, Culture and Recording: What makes participation accessible for this person?

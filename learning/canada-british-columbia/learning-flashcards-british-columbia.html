Learning Flashcards — British Columbia

Recall and explain

The eight decks contain five cards each. Choose a module, answer in your own words, then reveal the answer. Use the linked module and source library for legal detail; a short card cannot reproduce a statutory test.

Review difficult cards

Explain the distinction with a fictional example. Record uncertainty for supervision and return to the card after reading the source. The cards test learning and do not certify competence.

Revision cards

Professional Foundations — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking professional foundations in British Columbia?

Start with British Columbia College of Social Workers (BCCSW). Check the professional requirements for your role and the applicable law.

Professional Foundations — British Columbia: How does a formulation differ from a chronology?

It connects evidence, the person’s perspective and possible explanations to a reasoned plan.

Professional Foundations — British Columbia: How can supervision test bias?

Ask what evidence challenges your preferred explanation and whose perspective is missing.

Professional Foundations — British Columbia: What makes a professional record useful?

It distinguishes observations, reported information, analysis, actions and review responsibilities.

Professional Foundations — British Columbia: What makes reflective learning actionable?

A specific change to practice, a way to seek feedback and a date to revisit its impact.

Social Care and Support — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking social care and support in British Columbia?

Open BC government services from the module. Confirm scope, version and local procedures.

Social Care and Support — British Columbia: Can family support be assumed?

No. Explore willingness, ability and sustainability with each person involved.

Social Care and Support — British Columbia: Why distinguish assessment from funding?

A description of need does not itself determine eligibility, availability or costs under a particular programme.

Social Care and Support — British Columbia: What belongs in a support plan?

Desired outcomes, agreed actions, responsible people, contingency and review.

Social Care and Support — British Columbia: What if support is unavailable?

Record the unmet need, interim arrangements and escalation; do not record an unavailable service as an agreed solution.

Decision-Making and Capacity — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking decision-making and capacity in British Columbia?

Open BC Laws from the module. Confirm scope, version and local procedures.

Decision-Making and Capacity — British Columbia: Does a risky refusal alone establish inability to decide?

No. Explore the decision-making process, supports and applicable legal criteria.

Decision-Making and Capacity — British Columbia: What support can improve participation?

Accessible information, suitable timing, interpretation and communication aids chosen for the person.

Decision-Making and Capacity — British Columbia: Does a family relationship establish decision-making authority?

Do not assume it does. Verify the applicable law and any appointment or authorisation.

Decision-Making and Capacity — British Columbia: Why identify the exact decision?

Different decisions can involve different abilities, legal tests and representatives. Avoid a global label.

Restrictions and Liberty — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking restrictions and liberty in British Columbia?

Open BC Laws and BC mental-health law from the module. Confirm scope, version and local procedures.

Restrictions and Liberty — British Columbia: What should a restriction record describe?

Actual arrangements, duration, intensity, effects, ability to leave and the person’s account.

Restrictions and Liberty — British Columbia: Does quiet behaviour demonstrate consent?

Do not assume consent from silence. Explore wishes, understanding, pressure and communication.

Restrictions and Liberty — British Columbia: Can provider policy alone establish legal authority?

A policy must operate within applicable law. Identify the legal basis and scope of the proposed restriction.

Restrictions and Liberty — British Columbia: What should review consider?

Whether the restriction remains justified, less restrictive alternatives, objections and routes to challenge.

Mental Health — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking mental health in British Columbia?

Open BC mental-health law from the module. Confirm scope, version and local procedures.

Mental Health — British Columbia: Why distinguish admission from treatment?

The legal basis for admission may differ from the authority to provide a particular treatment.

Mental Health — British Columbia: What should happen alongside crisis assessment?

Attend to immediate health needs, communication, the person’s account and available support.

Mental Health — British Columbia: Can a social worker use every mental-health power?

No. Establish the qualifications, appointment and authority required for the particular role.

Mental Health — British Columbia: What does a rights explanation cover?

The proposed action, reasons, available advocacy, review and ways to raise concerns.

Adult Safeguarding — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking adult safeguarding in British Columbia?

Open BC adult protection legislation from the module. Confirm scope, version and local procedures.

Adult Safeguarding — British Columbia: Why offer a private conversation?

It may help identify coercion or harm that a person cannot safely describe in front of others.

Adult Safeguarding — British Columbia: Is proof needed before checking reporting duties?

Do not wait to prove abuse. Consider the applicable reporting threshold and act promptly when it is met.

Adult Safeguarding — British Columbia: What should a report contain?

Relevant facts, the concern, immediate safety needs, the person’s account and actions already taken.

Adult Safeguarding — British Columbia: Does a referral complete the response?

No. Confirm receipt, identify responsibility, review safety and escalate unresolved concerns as needed.

Children, Families and Transitions — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking children, families and transitions in British Columbia?

Open BC child protection from the module. Confirm scope, version and local procedures.

Children, Families and Transitions — British Columbia: How should the child’s account be recorded?

Use their own words where possible and distinguish what they said from adult interpretation.

Children, Families and Transitions — British Columbia: Can one child-protection model settle a legal threshold?

No. A practice model helps enquiry; reporting and intervention require the applicable legal analysis.

Children, Families and Transitions — British Columbia: What makes transition planning useful?

Named responsibilities and dates across services, guided by the young person’s goals and continuity of support.

Children, Families and Transitions — British Columbia: Why consider community and cultural relationships?

They may support identity, participation and safety. Ask the family rather than making assumptions.

Rights, Culture and Recording — British Columbia: Where should you begin checking rights, culture and recording in British Columbia?

Open BCCSW standards and resources from the module. Confirm scope, version and local procedures.

Rights, Culture and Recording — British Columbia: What is data minimisation?

Sharing only the information needed for the identified, lawful purpose.

Rights, Culture and Recording — British Columbia: What is a culturally responsive first step?

Ask the person how they want to communicate and which cultural or community supports they want involved.

Rights, Culture and Recording — British Columbia: Why record disagreement?

It preserves the person’s perspective and makes professional reasoning and review more accountable.

Rights, Culture and Recording — British Columbia: How do you compare alternatives?

Consider likely benefit, burdens, rights, the person’s preferences and whether a less intrusive option could work.

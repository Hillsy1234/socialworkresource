Case Route Finder — British Columbia

Use the route finder

Select the concerns that fit a fictional case. More than one framework may be relevant. Open each suggested module and its sources; the tool selects learning topics and does not decide a legal threshold.

Compare the routes

Separate support needs, decision-making, mental-health intervention, protective reporting and restrictions. Record the question each route answers, the source you checked and who needs to provide further advice.

Route questions

Care or carer support needs

Which needs and outcomes belong to the person and to carers?

Contact the appropriate health-authority home and community care service for eligibility and needs assessment. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

A decision-specific capacity question

What is the precise decision and why is ability to decide questioned?

Support the adult’s own decision and apply the relevant statutory incapability test. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Restrictions or liberty concerns

What restrictions actually operate and how do they affect the person?

Describe the restriction, who enforces it and the adult’s wishes or objections. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Mental health support or compulsion questions

What is the purpose of each proposed intervention?

Identify the route and evidence for each applicable criterion, including voluntary alternatives. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Abuse, neglect or safeguarding concerns

How can the person be heard safely and privately?

Identify the alleged harm and why the adult may be unable to seek assistance themselves. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

A child, young carer or transition issue

What does the child say or show about safety and daily life?

Identify the concern and the child’s age, using the CFCSA reporting threshold. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Language, participation or recording concerns

What makes participation accessible for this person?

Identify the service decision, relevant right and responsible organisation. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

Professional learning or supervision

What is the purpose and limit of your role?

Confirm the practitioner’s role, regulatory position and any specific statutory designation. Check territorial scope, current version, commencement and the relevant service procedure.

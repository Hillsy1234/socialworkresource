Pathway activities

Professional Foundations

Identify the employer, state, service setting and any appointment required for the task. Work through the fictional case and explain: Equating association membership, clinical experience or a job title with every statutory appointment.

Social Care and Support

Identify the scheme responsible for each need and the evidence required for its assessment. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating NDIS eligibility as approval for every requested service, or using obsolete aged-care programme rules.

Decision-Making and Capacity

Support the person to make the specific decision and assess the relevant decision-making ability. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating an instructional directive as merely optional, or assuming financial administration includes medical consent.

Restrictions and Liberty

Identify the actual restriction, setting and whether the disability, mental-health or aged-care framework applies. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating historical approval or a lodged plan as permanent permission, including for a prohibited practice.

Mental Health

Identify the stage, authorised practitioner and exact statutory criteria. Work through the fictional case and explain: Using the assessment and treatment criteria interchangeably or treating a support person as the treatment decision-maker.

Adult Safeguarding

Hear the adult safely and identify immediate danger, coercion and practical dependence. Work through the fictional case and explain: Treating all adult safeguarding as one statutory process or assuming a guardianship referral resolves family violence.

Children, Families and Transitions

Check the actual professional or employment category and the applicable reporting duty. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming all social-work job titles create the same reporting duty, or confusing voluntary support with a mandatory report.

Rights, Culture and Recording

Identify the relevant Charter rights and whether the decision-maker is a covered public authority. Work through the fictional case and explain: Assuming every private provider has identical Charter duties or that naming rights establishes proper consideration.

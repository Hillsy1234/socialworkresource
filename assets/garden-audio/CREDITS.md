# The Quiet Garden — recorded sound effects

All recordings below are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- `wood-1..4.mp3`, `grass-1..4.mp3`: Kenney, **Impact Sounds** (1.0, 2019), original `footstep_wood_000..003.ogg` and `footstep_grass_000..003.ogg`. https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- `gravel-1..5.mp3`: **42 Snow and Gravel Footsteps**, recordings by Corsica_S, extracted by Iwan Gabovitch (qubodup). Original `Corsica_S-Walking_on_snow_covered_gravel_01..05.flac`. The source explicitly grants CC0 permission for these extracts. https://opengameart.org/content/42-snow-and-gravel-footsteps
- `splash-1..3.mp3`: **6 Short water splashes**, recordings by ezwa, extracted by qubodup. Original `water_splash-01..03.flac`. https://opengameart.org/content/6-short-water-splashes
- `water-lap.mp3`: **Water Waves**, recording by transitking, extracted by qubodup. Original `wave_01_cc0-11505__transitking__wavesound.flac`. https://opengameart.org/content/water-waves

For the garden, recordings were trimmed, converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3, high-pass filtered at 65 Hz, peak-adjusted towards -6 dB and given short edge fades. The grass recordings also supply a quiet, high-pass-filtered foliage rustle. Playback varies gain, rate and stereo position slightly without changing the source licence. `manifest.json` records the exact original names and shipped file hashes.

The wind-like background, gentle rain bed and occasional birdsong are generated by Web Audio. They are not field recordings. Audio assets load from this website only after Sound is enabled; there is no external audio service or per-play fee.

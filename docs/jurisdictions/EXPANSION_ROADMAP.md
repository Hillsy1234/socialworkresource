# Jurisdiction expansion roadmap

All listed stages now form the final local learning-guide release. See [FINAL_RELEASE.md](FINAL_RELEASE.md) for editorial corrections, scope and verification. Independent practice review remains a separate recorded status and is not implied by the final release label.

The existing England and Wales packs remain the reference structure. New jurisdictions are added as independent manifests with their own modules, source register, flashcards, scenarios, templates, professional-development guidance and review metadata.

## Stage 1 — Scotland (implemented locally)

Build and validate the Scotland pack first. Cover Adult Support and Protection, Adults with Incapacity, Scottish mental-health law, self-directed support, carers, children’s hearings, rights, recording and Scottish Social Services Council practice standards. Keep Scottish statutory terms and routes separate from the Wales and England packs. The local pack is now implemented and browser-checked; independent Scottish practitioner review remains before publication.

## Stage 2 — Northern Ireland (implemented locally)

Add a separate Northern Ireland pack covering the Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, Mental Health Order, adult safeguarding, children’s safeguarding, Health and Social Care Trust pathways and Northern Ireland Social Care Council standards. The local pack is now implemented and validated; independent Northern Irish practitioner review remains before publication.

## Stage 3 — Republic of Ireland (implemented locally)

Add Ireland as a separate national system, not a UK variant. Cover CORU registration and professional conduct, the Assisted Decision-Making framework, HSE social-care pathways, child protection, domestic violence and Irish data-protection practice. The local pack is now implemented and validated; independent Irish practitioner review remains before publication.

## Stage 4 — New Zealand (implemented locally)

Add Aotearoa New Zealand with Social Workers Registration Board requirements, practising certificates, supervision and continuing professional development, Oranga Tamariki, health and disability pathways, Māori and whānau practice, and culturally safe language. The local pack is now implemented and validated; independent Aotearoa New Zealand practitioner review remains before publication.

## Stage 5 — Australia, Canada and the United States (initial sub-jurisdictions implemented locally)

These cannot safely be represented by one country-level legal pack. The first sub-jurisdictions are now implemented locally:

- Australia: New South Wales.
- Canada: Ontario.
- United States: California.
- Australia: Victoria.
- Canada: British Columbia.
- United States: New York.

Each state, province or territory must have its own law, regulator, safeguarding route, practice standards, privacy rules and sources.

## Shared release gate for every stage

1. Author the manifest and all 26 sections using the current structure.
2. Validate every cross-reference, download, route, tool and static reading page.
3. Check official sources, amendments, commencement dates and territorial scope.
4. Test country switching, search isolation, storage isolation, history, mobile layout, printing and failed-load recovery.
5. Obtain independent review from a practitioner or subject expert in that jurisdiction.
6. Only then change the pack metadata from authored/unreviewed to practice-reviewed and consider publication.

The app can support all five stages in the same codebase, but the content must remain country-specific. Selecting a country does not determine cross-border responsibility, ordinary residence, funding or court jurisdiction.

# Student and ASYE Pathway

This pathway is for social work students, newly qualified social workers, ASYEs, practice educators, and supervisors who want a structured route through the resource.

The aim is to build confidence gradually: values, assessment, capacity, liberty, mental health, safeguarding, families, equality, human rights, and recording.

## How to Use the Pathway

Work through one step at a time. After each section, write a short reflection:

- What is the key legal or practice idea?
- What would I need to evidence in a record?
- What would I ask in supervision?
- What would I check before using this in a live case?

## October 2026 case-law exercise

Compare TDB v Haringey with the two new scenarios. Explain when a capacity assessment is needed, why section 67 participation support is different, and why a deputy’s actual order matters. Then use Re BM and Re PK & MK to explain why contentment and absence of Article 5 do not end the safeguards analysis. Read the full case notes and their court-level limitations.

## Best Interests Meeting Agenda

```text
1. Decision to be made
2. Capacity conclusion and evidence
3. Person's wishes and feelings
4. Past wishes, beliefs, values
5. Options
6. Benefits and risks of each option
7. Less restrictive alternatives
8. Views of family/friends/carers/attorney/deputy/advocate
9. Professional recommendations
10. Decision-maker
11. Decision and rationale
12. Implementation plan
13. Review and challenge route
```

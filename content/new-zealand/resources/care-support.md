# Social care and support — Aotearoa New Zealand

Support may involve health and disability services, Whaikaha, local providers, whānau, iwi and community organisations. Identify the relevant service eligibility, needs assessment, funding and complaints pathway; there is no UK Care Act route.

Begin with the person’s and whānau’s goals, strengths, accessibility and cultural context. Do not assume unpaid whānau support is available or sustainable. Discuss self-determination, options, risks, safeguards and review.

# Safeguarding and family violence — Aotearoa New Zealand

Use the relevant health, disability, family-violence and child-protection policy for the service and circumstances. Establish immediate safety, the person’s account, coercion, cultural context, support network, information-sharing basis and desired outcomes.

Where tamariki are involved, contact Oranga Tamariki through the applicable pathway. Adults may need health, disability, police or family-violence responses. Do not invent a UK adult safeguarding threshold; record the New Zealand policy and local escalation route used.

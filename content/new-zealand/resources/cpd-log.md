# CPD and Reflection Log — Aotearoa New Zealand

## Reflect on learning

Name the activity, date, what you learned and how it could improve practice. Add feedback from supervision or a colleague and choose a next action.

## Keep your record

Reflections save in this browser for Aotearoa New Zealand. Export a text copy or print a PDF; browser clearing and device changes can remove local records. Do not enter identifiable case information. Saving here does not submit evidence to Social Workers Registration Board (SWRB). Check its own requirements separately.

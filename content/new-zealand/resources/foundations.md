# Professional Foundations — Aotearoa New Zealand

The Social Workers Registration Board (SWRB) Code of Conduct and ten Core Competence Standards anchor practice. Registered social workers must hold a current practising certificate, follow the Code, undertake CPD and practise within competence. The Code expects respect for Māori as tangata whenua, cultural safety, privacy, accurate records and responsible use of technology.

Use supervision to examine power, culture, language, bias, evidence and the person’s goals. Record what the person and whānau said, what you observed, your analysis, authority and review.

# Decision-making and capacity — Aotearoa New Zealand

Use the Protection of Personal and Property Rights Act 1988 and current New Zealand guidance for capacity, welfare guardianship, enduring powers and supported decision-making. Define the decision, support communication and identify the person’s will and preferences. Do not import UK MCA terminology or tests without checking local law.

Consider whānau involvement with the person’s authority, cultural safety, least restrictive options, advocacy, evidence and review. Seek legal or specialist advice where guardianship, compulsory treatment or liberty restrictions arise.

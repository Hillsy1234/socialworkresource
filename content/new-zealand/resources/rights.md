# Rights, culture and recording — Aotearoa New Zealand

Use the SWRB Code, cultural competence, Te Tiriti o Waitangi responsibilities, the New Zealand Disability Strategy and human-rights protections. Ask how the person communicates, what cultural or language support is wanted and who should be involved.

Record the person’s and whānau’s views, authority, evidence, alternatives, proportionality, disagreement and review. Use technology safely and protect privacy.

[SWRB Code of Conduct](https://swrb.govt.nz/practice/code-of-conduct/)

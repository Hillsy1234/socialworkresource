# Restrictions and liberty — Aotearoa New Zealand

There is no single New Zealand DoLS scheme equivalent to England or Wales. Examine the actual restriction, consent, statutory authority, purpose, duration, necessity, proportionality and ability to challenge. Consider the Protection of Personal and Property Rights Act, mental-health law, disability rights and provider policy.

Include the person and whānau, provide accessible information and record less restrictive alternatives. A diagnosis, risk label or provider policy does not itself authorise confinement.

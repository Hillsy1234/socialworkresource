# Mental health — Aotearoa New Zealand

The Mental Health (Compulsory Assessment and Treatment) Act 1992 and current Ministry of Health guidance govern compulsory assessment and treatment. Identify the purpose, responsible clinicians, rights advice, independent advocacy, cultural support and review route. Check current reform information before applying a provision.

Separate compulsory mental-health treatment from capacity, disability support, safeguarding and everyday care. Engage whānau appropriately and respect Māori and Pacific cultural contexts. Record why intervention is necessary and what less restrictive options were considered.

# Tamariki, rangatahi and whānau — Aotearoa New Zealand

Oranga Tamariki is the statutory child-care and protection agency. Use the Oranga Tamariki Practice Framework, the Oranga Tamariki Act 1989 and current guidance. Work with tamariki, parents, whānau, hapū and iwi; whānau searching and culturally grounded assessment are core practice considerations.

Listen to the child, explain confidentiality, record facts separately from analysis and coordinate education, health, disability, family support and safety. Consider whakapapa, identity and placement relationships without stereotyping.

# Quick Reference — British Columbia

## Before choosing an action

1. Hear the person and clarify immediate safety.
2. Identify the specific decision, service, setting and jurisdiction.
3. Check authority, consent, reporting duties and privacy.
4. Compare support and less restrictive alternatives.
5. Record evidence, the person’s views, action owners and review.

Check the official source’s current version, commencement and territorial scope. This quick reference cannot determine thresholds or timescales for a live case.

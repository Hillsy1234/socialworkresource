# Decision-Making and Capacity — British Columbia

## British Columbia context

Locate the Health Care (Consent) and Care Facility (Admission) Act and Representation Agreement Act. Distinguish a representation agreement, substitute consent and guardianship; confirm the authority for the actual decision.

## Practice enquiry

Decision support, consent and the authority for someone else to decide. Start with the person’s account and distinguish information received from observations and interpretation.

- What is the precise decision and why is ability to decide questioned?
- What communication support would help?
- Who has authority for this decision under the applicable law?

## Recording and review

1. Decision and timing.
2. Communication and participation support.
3. Person’s account and reasons.
4. Applicable legal test and evidence.
5. Scope of any representative’s authority.
6. Disagreement, challenge and review.

Explain the reasons for the proposed action, record disagreement and identify who will review the outcome. Check the local legal criteria before treating any learning prompt as a duty or power.

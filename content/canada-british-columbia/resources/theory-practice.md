# Theory Into Practice — British Columbia

## Build a testable explanation

Use the theory finder to generate alternative explanations for a fictional case. Compare what each lens notices about relationships, environment, power, trauma and strengths. Record evidence that would support or challenge your explanation.

## Use models thoughtfully

Theories are shared learning lenses, not country-specific statutory rules. Their inclusion does not imply official adoption in British Columbia. Check suitability, training requirements, cultural context and the person’s account before applying a method.

# Practice Scenarios — British Columbia

## Work through a fictional case

Read the scenario, identify the person’s perspective and answer the three questions before revealing the discussion. Compare your reasoning with the worked response and open the suggested modules.

## Check your reasoning

The cases are fictional and the discussions are learning examples. Establish the British Columbia source and local route for any legal conclusion. Consider what evidence is missing, alternative explanations, responsibility for next actions and how outcomes will be reviewed.

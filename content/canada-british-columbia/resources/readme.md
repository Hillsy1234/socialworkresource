# Start Here — British Columbia

## Your British Columbia learning guide

Use the location selector to choose among twelve separate guides. Your reading progress, confidence and reflections are stored separately for each location.

## Start learning

Read [Professional Foundations](/?jurisdiction=canada-british-columbia&resource=foundations#learningWorkspace), choose a topic from [Learning Map](/?jurisdiction=canada-british-columbia&resource=map#learningWorkspace), then practise with [Practice Scenarios](/?jurisdiction=canada-british-columbia&resource=scenarios#learningWorkspace). Test recall with flashcards and use the reflection log to identify a specific next action.

## Scope

Eight modules, forty flashcards, a glossary, eight fictional scenarios and ten recording prompts support revision and supervision. This is a final learning-guide release. It is not an exhaustive account of law, a statutory form or a regulator-approved training programme. The source library explains how to check the applicable version and local route.

# Restrictions and liberty — Ireland

Ireland does not use the England, Wales or Northern Ireland DoLS routes. Examine the lawful basis for any restriction, the Assisted Decision-Making (Capacity) framework, relevant health or residential-care law, constitutional and human-rights protections, consent, objection, necessity and proportionality.

Describe the actual arrangements, support to communicate, review and challenge route. Do not treat a diagnosis, a capacity finding or a provider policy as authority for confinement. Seek current legal and safeguarding advice for the setting.

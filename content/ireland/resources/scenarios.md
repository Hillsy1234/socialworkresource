# Scenario Workouts — Ireland practice guide


All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.


## Professional Foundations — Ireland

A worker is asked to describe a client as refusing support, although the only offer conflicts with the client’s religious practice and transport needs.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Record the actual offer and the reasons it was unsuitable. Explore an accessible alternative and bring any unresolved resource issue to supervision. A professional formulation should make the barrier visible rather than treating the organisation’s first offer as the only reasonable option.


## Social Care and Support — Ireland

An older person is ready to leave hospital, but the family is told that approved home-support funding means daily visits are already in place.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Confirm the provider, tasks, hours and first visit. Assess whether an interim plan is safe and agreed if staffing is unavailable. Keep the person’s preference for home, the family’s actual availability and any unmet need in the discharge record.


## Decision-Making and Capacity — Ireland

A person has a decision-making assistance agreement and wants to refuse a proposed move. Staff ask the assistant to approve it instead.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Support the person to consider the actual options and record their wishes. Read the agreement: assistance is not replacement decision-making. If a separate assessment or court issue arises, identify it explicitly rather than expanding the assistant’s role.


## Restrictions and Liberty — Ireland

A residential service says an adult cannot leave because the family signed the admission form. The adult repeatedly asks to go home.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Hear the adult with suitable support, establish the actual restrictions and examine the legal basis. Family agreement is not enough. Explore a workable alternative and obtain timely legal advice under the current consent policy about the necessary court or other lawful route.


## Mental Health — Ireland

A team circulates a summary of the newly enacted Act and proposes to use its future procedures for an admission immediately.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Check whether the relevant provisions and forms have commenced. Use the operative law and current MHC instructions for the admission. Record the version check and keep preparation for reform separate from the person’s present legal safeguards.


## Adult Safeguarding — Ireland

An adult says a paid supporter controls their bank card but asks that the supporter not be dismissed because no replacement is available.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Explore coercion and immediate access to money, food and support. Make the appropriate safeguarding response while planning continuity of assistance. Consider whether a criminal, provider or decision-support concern also needs action; do not reduce the plan to removing one worker.


## Children, Families and Transitions — Ireland

A child describes repeated lack of food and supervision. A worker is told to wait until the next team meeting to establish proof.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Assess immediate safety and the evidence of harm, use the relevant Tusla reporting threshold and act without waiting to prove the allegation. Record the child’s account accurately and arrange practical support alongside the protection response.


## Rights, Culture and Recording — Ireland

An agency requests a complete family file to decide whether to offer a transport service.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Clarify the purpose and the minimum information actually required, the lawful basis and any sensitive-data condition. Discuss participation and confidentiality with the person where appropriate. Record what is shared and why, rather than sending the file simply because the requester is another public body.

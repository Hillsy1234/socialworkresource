# Social care and support — Ireland

Irish social work and social care pathways are delivered through HSE services, disability and community providers, Tusla and other agencies. Identify the responsible service, the person’s outcomes, eligibility or assessment process, family and carer needs, advocacy and complaints route. Do not import a UK Care Act threshold.

Separate needs assessment, service planning, funding and review. Record communication, cultural identity, consent, disagreement, contingency and who will act. Check current HSE policy and local service arrangements.

# Children and families — Ireland

Use Children First: National Guidance for the Protection and Welfare of Children 2017, the Children First Act 2015 and Tusla procedures. Mandated persons and relevant-service providers have specific obligations, including reporting and child safeguarding statements.

Listen to the child, explain confidentiality and reporting in an accessible way, record facts separately from analysis and coordinate Tusla, An Garda Síochána, education, health and family support as appropriate.

[Children First guidance and legislation](https://www.tusla.ie/children-first/children-first-guidance-and-legislation/)

# Decision-making and capacity — Ireland

The Assisted Decision-Making (Capacity) Act 2015, as amended and commenced on 26 April 2023, provides a functional, issue- and time-specific framework. It established decision-support arrangements and the Decision Support Service, replacing the former wardship approach over the transition period.

Support the person to decide, identify the least restrictive support arrangement and check the authority of a decision-making assistant, co-decision-maker, decision-making representative, advance healthcare directive or other arrangement. Check current regulations and Decision Support Service guidance.

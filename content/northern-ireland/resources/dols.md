# Deprivation of liberty — Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland uses the Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 framework for deprivation of liberty, including authorisation and review safeguards. Describe the actual arrangements, purpose, duration, intensity, freedom to leave, consent, objection and the person’s wishes.

The nominated person is not the decision-maker but must be consulted in the circumstances set out in the Act. Check the current Code, forms, Trust pathway and Tribunal rights. Consider less restrictive alternatives and record how the person can challenge the authorisation.

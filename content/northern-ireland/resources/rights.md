# Rights, participation and recording — Northern Ireland

Use NISCC standards, human-rights and equality duties, accessible information and local complaints or advocacy routes. Record the person’s wishes, communication support, legal or policy basis, alternatives, proportionality, disagreement and review.

Northern Ireland has its own Trust and Department of Health pathways. Check whether information sharing is lawful, necessary and proportionate, and explain decisions in a way the person can understand.

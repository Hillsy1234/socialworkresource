# Practice Glossary — Northern Ireland

## Use terms in context

Search the glossary, read a definition and open the linked module. These are learning definitions. Statutory terms must be read in their own law and jurisdiction.

## Check understanding

Explain a term in ordinary language and give an example. Distinguish everyday words such as consent, capacity and eligibility from their specific legal meanings. Consult Northern Ireland Social Care Council (NISCC) for professional terminology within its remit.

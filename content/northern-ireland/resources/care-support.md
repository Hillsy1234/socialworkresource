# Social care and support — Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland’s health and social care is organised through five Health and Social Care Trusts and the Department of Health. Identify the Trust pathway, community-care assessment, domiciliary or residential support, carers’ needs and local eligibility or charging arrangements. There is no Northern Ireland Care Act equivalent.

Start with the person’s outcomes, communication, family and community support. Distinguish assessment, service decision, funding and review. Do not assume a relative’s willingness or treat a service list as an outcomes-based plan. Record advocacy, disagreements, contingency and who will act.

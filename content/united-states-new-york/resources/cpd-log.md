# CPD and Reflection Log — New York

## Reflect on learning

Name the activity, date, what you learned and how it could improve practice. Add feedback from supervision or a colleague and choose a next action.

## Keep your record

Reflections save in this browser for New York. Export a text copy or print a PDF; browser clearing and device changes can remove local records. Do not enter identifiable case information. Saving here does not submit evidence to New York State Education Department, Office of the Professions. Check its own requirements separately.

# Social Care and Support — New York

## New York context

Identify the local social services district, health programme or housing service responsible for the proposed support. Clarify applications, eligibility, accessibility and review without assuming that one programme covers all needs.

## Practice enquiry

Needs, strengths, access to support, family responsibilities and service planning. Start with the person’s account and distinguish information received from observations and interpretation.

- Which needs and outcomes belong to the person and to carers?
- Which service actually assesses and funds this support?
- What happens if the proposed support is unavailable?

## Recording and review

1. Person’s goals and daily living.
2. Strengths and environmental barriers.
3. Family willingness and sustainability.
4. Eligibility and service access.
5. Costs considered separately.
6. Plan, contingency and review.

Explain the reasons for the proposed action, record disagreement and identify who will review the outcome. Check the local legal criteria before treating any learning prompt as a duty or power.

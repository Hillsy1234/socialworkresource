# CPD and Reflection Log

## Reflect on learning and impact
Social Care Wales announced changes to CPD recording from 1 April 2025, removing the former hours targets and the requirement to log details in SCWonline. Regular manager discussions remain part of its guidance, and records may be sampled. [Read the regulator’s CPD update](https://socialcare.wales/news-stories/weve-changed-our-cpd-requirements-to-make-it-simpler).

## Your private learning record
Use the form below for your own reflections. It does not submit to a regulator or confirm renewal requirements. Keep identifying case details out of notes. Browser storage can be cleared or unavailable, so export important records.

## Separate country records
Switching countries keeps your England and Wales entries separate. An unfinished reflection is saved locally as you type when browser storage is available.

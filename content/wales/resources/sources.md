## How to check a source

Record the title, issuing body, territorial scope, publication/update date and date you consulted it. Check whether a legal amendment is in force and whether guidance has been replaced. Read the actual provision and relevant Code in context. Compare local procedures with the statutory framework and escalate unresolved conflict. Linked older forms may remain available online without incorporating later case law; verify their current use.

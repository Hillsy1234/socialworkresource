# Wales quick reference

## Care and support

Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act: Parts 3, 4 and 5 address assessment, meeting needs and charging. Sections 19, 21 and 24 distinguish adult, child and carer assessments. Check the relevant regulations and Code; separate needs, eligibility and finances.

## Capacity

Define the decision and time. Provide practicable support. Apply the statutory test and record evidence. If capacity is lacking, complete the separate best interests process; identify any further authority needed for restrictions.

## Liberty

Describe the actual arrangements and person’s wishes, consent and objection. Use the June 2026 judgment and current Welsh guidance. Establish the appropriate DoLS or court route for the setting and age.

## Mental health

Identify the purpose of the proposed intervention and involve the relevant clinicians and AMHP. Use the Welsh Code and check current provisions and commencement. Coordinate care under the relevant Welsh Measure arrangements.

## Adult safeguarding

Apply the section 126 adult-at-risk framework, Welsh reporting procedures and relevant section 128 duty. Attend to immediate safety, hear the adult safely and record outcomes, actions and review.

## Children and transition

Use Wales Safeguarding Procedures, Welsh care/support and ALN/IDP frameworks. Coordinate around the child or young person’s own account, continuity and named responsibilities.

## Rights and recording

Make an active offer of Welsh-language support. Distinguish evidence, views and analysis. Explain lawful, proportionate actions and information sharing, disagreements and review.

## Professional learning

Use the current Social Care Wales Code and CPD guidance. Reflect on impact and discussion; this local log does not submit to the regulator.

# Wales printable practice tools

Ten downloadable prompts cover care and support, carers, capacity, best interests, safeguarding, liberty, mental health, transition, supervision and chronology. Every file identifies Wales and its source.

## Using a prompt

1. Choose the relevant topic; more than one may apply.
2. Practise with a fictional case.
3. Identify unanswered questions and current-source checks.
4. Review your reasoning in supervision.
5. Use your organisation’s approved records for actual casework.

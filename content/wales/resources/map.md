# Wales learning map

Use one topic per study session or week. Read the module, test recall with its flashcards, complete a fictional scenario and record one action in the reflection log.

## 1. Professional foundations

Write a case formulation distinguishing facts, the person’s account and your analysis.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

## 2. Care and carer support

Assess a fictional adult and carer separately; distinguish needs, eligibility and financial assessment.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

## 3. Capacity and decisions

Define one decision, provide support and explain the evidence for your conclusion.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

## 4. Liberty and rights

Describe restrictions and wishes; identify the current source and authorisation question.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

## 5. Mental health routes

Compare the purpose and legal basis of mental health and physical healthcare interventions.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

## 6. Adult safeguarding

Apply the section 126 framework to the Rhys scenario and plan safe reporting.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

## 7. Children and transition

Map an IDP and care/support transition plan around the young person’s voice.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

## 8. Rights and reflection

Record language support, disagreement, proportionate action and a review date.

Evidence to bring to supervision: a short anonymised written response, one uncertainty and the official source checked.

# Start Here — Wales

## Your Wales learning workspace

Choose where you practise above. Twelve practice locations each have their own content, professional context and separately saved learning progress and reflections. The guide supports learning, revision and supervision; it does not make case decisions.

## What is included

- Eight learning modules with official sources and practical exercises.
- Forty flashcards and a searchable glossary of 28 terms.
- A case route finder, theory/hypothesis tool and children’s practice-model finder.
- Eight worked scenarios and ten downloadable recording prompts.
- A student and newly qualified pathway, learning map and quick reference.
- A private Wales reflection log with save, export and print.

## Begin with the person

Start with what matters to the person, their language and communication, relationships, strengths, needs and concerns. Identify the applicable duties, test assumptions against evidence and agree a plan with named responsibilities and review. For cross-border work, establish responsibilities explicitly: the selector does not decide ordinary residence, funding or court jurisdiction.

## Learning sequence

Read Professional Foundations, then follow the Learning Map. Use a fictional scenario to practise and a reflection to identify your next action. Mark sections read and rate confidence to focus later revision.

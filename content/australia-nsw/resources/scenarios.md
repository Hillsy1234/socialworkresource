# Scenario Workouts — New South Wales practice guide


All cases are fictional learning exercises. Explain your evidence and reasoning before reading the discussion.


## Professional Foundations — New South Wales

A practitioner is asked to sign a statutory assessment because they hold an AASW credential, without checking the specific appointment requirements.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Read the law and service procedure governing that assessment. Confirm qualifications, appointment and scope before signing. Bring any gap to the responsible manager and arrange an authorised practitioner while continuing work that is within your role.


## Social Care and Support — New South Wales

A person is told that their NDIS plan should pay for every need after a hospital admission, including treatment and unsuitable housing.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Map the actual support sought and the responsibility of each system. Obtain the relevant decisions and coordinate health, housing and disability support. Record unmet needs and interim arrangements rather than presenting an unfunded referral as a completed plan.


## Decision-Making and Capacity — New South Wales

A daughter who manages bills offers consent for a procedure, while the patient objects and an appointed guardian has a medical-consent function.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Check the patient’s decision-making ability and objection, then the guardian’s actual authority and the treatment category. The daughter’s financial help does not settle the consent route. Obtain the appropriate specialist or NCAT advice rather than collecting whichever signature is easiest.


## Restrictions and Liberty — New South Wales

An NDIS provider locks kitchen cupboards and says the uploaded behaviour plan is all the permission it needs.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Identify the restriction’s purpose and effect, examine the NSW authorisation requirements and check the person’s participation. Consider less restrictive access and support. Resolve missing approvals and any incident duties with the responsible practitioners; an uploaded plan does not resolve them.


## Mental Health — New South Wales

A person declines admission but accepts a crisis-team visit and support from a friend. Staff describe the refusal as sufficient reason for detention.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Assess the harm evidence and whether the offered alternative is suitable and available. Ask the authorised clinician to apply the relevant criteria and record the reasoning. The refusal is one fact to understand, not a complete statutory assessment.


## Adult Safeguarding — New South Wales

An older person reports that a relative takes their money, while a provider separately fails to deliver agreed meals.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Map the financial exploitation and the service failure as distinct concerns. Arrange immediate food access and safe contact, consider ADC and any police response, and pursue the provider or regulator pathway for missed care. Check who has accepted each action.


## Children, Families and Transitions — New South Wales

A worker hears a child describe violence at home and is told that a referral to a family-support charity is sufficient.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Assess immediate safety and the significant-harm concern, check the reporting duty and use the appropriate DCJ or police route. Family support may be valuable alongside that action, but it cannot be used to avoid an applicable statutory report.


## Rights, Culture and Recording — New South Wales

A provider proposes sending a complete health record to a housing worker because both organisations support the same client.


- Which local framework and decision-maker apply?
- What evidence and support could change the next step?
- How will the person participate, challenge and obtain review?


### Discussion

Clarify the housing decision, the relevant information and the applicable privacy framework. Seek or identify the appropriate legal basis, respect confidentiality limits and record what is shared. The shared client does not make unrestricted file exchange necessary.

# Rights, Culture and Recording — California

## California context

Use BBS requirements for clinical social-work registration, licensure and supervision. Apply the relevant state confidentiality law and federal privacy rules where applicable; HIPAA is not a universal rule for every social-work record.

## Practice enquiry

Participation, privacy, cultural safety and clear recording. Start with the person’s account and distinguish information received from observations and interpretation.

- What makes participation accessible for this person?
- What legal basis permits disclosure of this information?
- How will disagreement and reasons be recorded?

## Recording and review

1. Language and communication preferences.
2. Cultural support requested.
3. Consent and confidentiality limits.
4. Evidence separated from interpretation.
5. Alternatives and proportionality.
6. Person’s disagreement and review.

Explain the reasons for the proposed action, record disagreement and identify who will review the outcome. Check the local legal criteria before treating any learning prompt as a duty or power.

# Student and Newly Qualified Pathway — California

## Plan your learning

Work through the eight activities with a practice educator or supervisor. Use fictional examples, identify sources and explain your reasoning before looking at the worked scenario.

## Evidence of development

For each activity record one learning point, feedback received and a change to try. These exercises do not replace local qualification, placement, registration or continuing-development requirements. Consult California Board of Behavioral Sciences (BBS) and your education provider for those requirements.

# Learning Map — California

## A sequence for study

1. [Professional Foundations](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=foundations#learningWorkspace): Professional role, ethics, competence, supervision and accountable decisions.
2. [Social Care and Support](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=care-support#learningWorkspace): Needs, strengths, access to support, family responsibilities and service planning.
3. [Decision-Making and Capacity](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=mca#learningWorkspace): Decision support, consent and the authority for someone else to decide.
4. [Restrictions and Liberty](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=dols#learningWorkspace): Restrictions, legal authority, less restrictive options and review.
5. [Mental Health](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=mha#learningWorkspace): Mental-health support, crisis response, treatment and rights.
6. [Adult Safeguarding](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=safeguarding#learningWorkspace): Abuse, neglect, coercion, safe enquiry and the appropriate protective response.
7. [Children, Families and Transitions](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=children#learningWorkspace): Child participation, family support, child protection and transitions.
8. [Rights, Culture and Recording](/?jurisdiction=united-states-california&resource=rights#learningWorkspace): Participation, privacy, cultural safety and clear recording.

## Put learning into practice

Choose one fictional scenario. Record what is known, the person’s perspective and what still needs checking. Open the source library before answering a legal question. Use supervision to test alternative explanations and a reflection to record the next action.

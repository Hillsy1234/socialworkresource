# Children, Families and Transitions — Ontario

## Ontario context

Use the Child, Youth and Family Services Act 2017 and Ontario reporting guidance. Identify the appropriate children’s aid society and the reporting responsibility that applies; a discussion with a manager does not determine whether a statutory duty has been met.

## Practice enquiry

Child participation, family support, child protection and transitions. Start with the person’s account and distinguish information received from observations and interpretation.

- What does the child say or show about safety and daily life?
- Which reporting duty and receiving agency apply?
- How will education, health and family support coordinate?

## Recording and review

1. Child’s voice and communication.
2. Observed harm, needs and strengths.
3. Family, community and cultural relationships.
4. Local reporting requirements and response.
5. Education, health and support plan.
6. Transition, responsibility and review.

Explain the reasons for the proposed action, record disagreement and identify who will review the outcome. Check the local legal criteria before treating any learning prompt as a duty or power.

# CPD and Reflection Log — Ontario

## Reflect on learning

Name the activity, date, what you learned and how it could improve practice. Add feedback from supervision or a colleague and choose a next action.

## Keep your record

Reflections save in this browser for Ontario. Export a text copy or print a PDF; browser clearing and device changes can remove local records. Do not enter identifiable case information. Saving here does not submit evidence to Ontario College of Social Workers and Social Service Workers (OCSWSSW). Check its own requirements separately.

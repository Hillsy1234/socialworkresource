# Templates and Checklists — Ontario

## Recording prompts

Choose the prompt for the question you are exploring. Each download contains prompts and sources for Ontario; it is not an official form. The eight topic records are accompanied by supervision and source-check records.

## Use a prompt

Work with fictional information. Separate the person’s words, evidence and analysis. Add a source for legal assertions, explain disagreement and name responsibilities and review. For live work use the authorised record system and statutory forms required by your service.

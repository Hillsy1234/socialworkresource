# Printable Tools — Victoria

## Download and use the tools

The ten prompts cover eight practice topics, supervision and checking sources. Use them individually or export the collection from [Templates and Checklists](/?jurisdiction=australia-victoria&resource=templates#learningWorkspace).

## Before sharing

Check that the location is Victoria, use anonymised or fictional information, and keep personal learning notes separate from service records. These prompts help organise reasoning; they do not establish legal authority or meet a regulator’s submission requirements automatically.

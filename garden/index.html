AT YOUR OWN PACE

Make yourself at home.

Walk: W A S D or the arrow keys move you. Drag the view to look around. On a phone, hold the arrow buttons and drag the scene with your other finger. Q and E turn the view with a keyboard.

Follow the path: let the garden guide you. You can still look around. Pause at any time, or press Escape. Moving yourself stops the guided walk.

Take a seat: “Sit a while” pauses the walk and lowers your viewpoint. The map and “Next quiet spot” let you move instantly between six places.

Still mode: pauses walking, water and foliage. Look around using the map, or switch it off to walk again. It starts on if your device requests reduced motion.

Weather: choose clear skies, cloud, light rain or mist independently of the lighting. Optional automatic weather changes gently during your visit. Still mode freezes weather and pauses automatic changes.

Sound is optional: footsteps follow the ground beneath you, with soft foliage rustles and water beside the pond. Garden preferences stay in this browser; no account is needed. Your original miniature garden is also available.

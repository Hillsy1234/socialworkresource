AT YOUR OWN PACE

Garden help & FAQs

Choose a question for a quick guide. Opening this panel pauses your walk.

How do I hide the controls and bring them back?

Select Just enjoy the garden. A guided walk or two-minute break keeps going while the panels disappear. A brief message explains how to return, and a small Show controls button stays visible.

Tap or click anywhere, or select Show controls, to bring the panels back without stopping the guided walk. Esc brings them back and pauses. Moving the mouse keeps the screen clear. If you are seated or in Still mode, you stay where you are.

How do I walk and look around?

Select Follow the path for a guided walk. For free walking, use W A S D or the arrow keys; Q / E turns you. Drag the scene to look around. On a phone, hold the on-screen arrows to walk and drag the view to look.

Choose Slow, Gentle or Steady from Pace. Adjust Look sensitivity in Your visit if turning feels too fast or slow.

How do I pause or stop a walk?

Select Pause, select the active Pause the walk button, or press Esc. Opening settings or these FAQs pauses the walk too. If you are using a keyboard to walk, release the movement keys to stop.

When you are ready, select Follow the path to continue. Hiding the controls with Just enjoy the garden keeps a guided walk running.

How do I throw a pebble into the pond?

Walk near the pond, or choose The water garden on the Map. Select Toss a pebble, then tap or click a reachable spot on the water. You can also select Gentle toss for a suggested landing point.

The pebble rises into view from below you, tumbles through the air and makes a small splash and ripples. There is no hand covering the view. Dragging still looks around. Select Put it down or press Esc to cancel aiming.

Why is a pebble throw unavailable or blocked?

Tossing is available near the pond with Still mode off. Look toward open water and choose a closer spot if a throw is blocked by the bank or bridge, or is beyond a gentle throw.

One pebble is active at a time. Wait for it to sink before tossing another. You can throw while standing or seated near the pond.

Can I sit down or jump to another place?

Select Sit a while to rest on a nearby bench, or at a lower viewpoint where you are. Select Stand up to get up again.

The Map takes you directly to any of nine places. Next quiet spot takes you to the next stop along the path. These options also work in Still mode.

What happens during the two-minute break?

Select 2-minute break for a guided section of the path that ends seated at the woodland bench. You can pause at any point or select End garden break.

Select Just enjoy the garden if you want the break to continue with the controls hidden. The break is unavailable in Still mode.

What does Still mode do?

Still mode stops continuous walking and freezes the garden’s visual animation, including ripples, weather and wildlife. It also pauses automatic weather and daylight changes and cancels pebble tossing.

You can still choose places on the Map and change the atmosphere. Still mode starts on if your device requests reduced motion. Sound is controlled separately with Sound on / off.

How do I turn sound on or adjust it?

Sound starts off on every new visit. Select Sound off to enable it. In Sound balance, adjust nature and footsteps, plus birdsong, wind and leaves, rain and roof patter, and pond and stream sounds.

Set a slider to Off to silence that layer, or switch Sound off to mute everything. Splash sounds play only when sound is enabled.

Can I change the weather, season and lighting?

Open Weather to choose clear skies, cloud, light rain or mist, and select a season. Use Morning, Golden hour or Evening in the toolbar to change the lighting.

Optional automatic weather changes gently during your visit. Let daylight move toward evening gradually changes the light, then rests at evening. Still mode pauses these automatic changes.

Where are the reading nook and breathing guide?

Choose The reading nook on the Map. Its roof shelters you from rain, and A few quiet words opens short original readings.

Breathe by the pond takes you to a seated view with an optional gentle breathing guide. Follow at your own comfortable pace, or close it whenever you like.

Can I save my favourite spot and settings?

Open Your visit and select Save this quiet spot. It remembers the place, whether you are seated, its season, weather, lighting and sound balance. Use Return to my spot to revisit it or Forget my spot to remove it.

Preferences stay in this browser; no account is needed and they do not sync between devices. Clearing browser storage can remove them. Sound still starts off when you return.

What if the garden feels slow or will not load?

In Your visit, try Automatic or Lower battery use under Display preference. Still mode offers a stationary view. If the 3D view cannot load, use the retry option or return to your learning.

The original Miniature view is also linked at the bottom of the garden.

How do I return to my learning?

Bring the controls back if they are hidden, then select Back to your learning at the top left. When you entered from a learning resource, this returns you to that resource.

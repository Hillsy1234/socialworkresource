WELCOME TO YOUR QUIET CORNER

A garden with nothing to prove.

Touch the six glowing flowers to guide your light through the garden. You can also use “Find a flower”, with a mouse, touch or keyboard.

Drag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom. In Still mode the scene and camera stay still; flowers open instantly.

Choose Arrange to add flowers, stones or lanterns. “Place for me” gives you the same experience without using the 3D canvas. Your garden saves only in this browser.

Sound starts only when you choose it. You can leave at any time; your learning page will be waiting.
